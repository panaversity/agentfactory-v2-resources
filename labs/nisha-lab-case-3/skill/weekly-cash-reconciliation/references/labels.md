# Labels for a difference

Use exactly one of these labels for each unmatched line.

| Label | Use it when | Does it fix itself? |
|---|---|---|
| Timing: deposit not yet cleared | A receipt is in the ledger and is not yet on the bank's record | Yes, usually within a few days |
| Timing: check not yet cashed | A check is in the ledger and is not yet on the bank's record | Yes, when the payee cashes it |
| Bank item to record | The bank shows a fee or interest that is not in the ledger | No. The ledger needs an entry. |
| Ledger error | A ledger line has the wrong amount or date for a matched bank line | No. The ledger needs a correction. |
| Suspected duplicate | Two bank payments look like the same bill paid twice | No. It needs a source document. If confirmed, a refund is asked for. |
| Open item | Anything else that no document explains yet | No. Someone has to find out. |

A timing label is only for a line that is in the ledger and not yet at the bank. A line that is at the bank and not in the ledger is never a timing item.
