## Write-down and its reversal

Last year, finished cloth was written down by Rs 6.0 million, because cloth prices had fallen below cost.

This year, prices recovered. As IAS 2 requires, the write-down was reversed, up to the original cost. The reversal of Rs 6.0 million was added to profit this year, shown in the account "Reversal of inventory write-down".

Inventories are measured at the lower of cost and net realizable value, using the first-in, first-out method.
