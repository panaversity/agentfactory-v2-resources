# Sutlej Weavers: note on leases

*Made-up data for teaching. Amounts in Rs million. Prepared under IFRS.*

## Warehouse lease

Sutlej rents a warehouse in Faisalabad on a five-year lease that began on the first day of this year.

- Rent: Rs 10.0 million a year, paid at the end of each year
- Term: five years
- Interest rate used to measure the lease: 8% a year
- What happens at the end of the lease, including whether ownership of the warehouse can pass to Sutlej, is set out in the lease contract. The contract is not included in this pack.

Under US GAAP, a lease is either an operating lease or a finance lease. Which one it is depends on the contract's terms, for example whether ownership passes to the tenant at the end of the lease.
