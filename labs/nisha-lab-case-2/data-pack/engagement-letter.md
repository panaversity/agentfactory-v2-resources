# Engagement letter

*Made-up data for teaching. All names are fictional.*

**From:** Mark Ellis, Finance Director, Northfield Home Goods (United States)
**To:** Nisha
**Subject:** IFRS to US GAAP bridge for Sutlej Weavers

Dear Nisha,

Northfield is considering buying a share in Sutlej Weavers, a textile exporter in Faisalabad, Pakistan. Sutlej reports under IFRS. Our board reports under US GAAP.

**The job.** A short bridge from Sutlej's IFRS profit before tax to US GAAP profit before tax, for three areas only: inventory, leases and development costs. We do not need a full conversion.

**What we know so far.** Sutlej rents its main warehouse for Rs 12.0 million a year. Its development work is on a water-saving dyeing process.

**Deadline.** Thursday, 6 p.m. New York time. Our board meets on Friday at 9 a.m. New York time.

**Fee.** US$2,500, fixed, paid on delivery.

Please send your work to me by email.

Mark Ellis
