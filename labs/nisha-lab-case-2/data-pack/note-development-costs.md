# Sutlej Weavers: note on development costs

*Made-up data for teaching. Amounts in Rs million. Prepared under IFRS.*

Sutlej is developing a water-saving process for dyeing cloth.

## This year

Rs 15.0 million was spent on development this year. All the tests in IAS 38 were met, so the full amount was recorded as an intangible asset, not as an expense.

## Earlier years

In earlier years, Rs 9.0 million of development costs was recorded as an asset. Amortization of these costs began this year, over three years. Rs 3.0 million was charged as an expense this year, in the account "Amortization of development costs".

## The asset at the year end

| Item | Rs million |
|---|---|
| Costs recorded this year | 15.0 |
| Costs from earlier years | 9.0 |
| Less: amortization this year | minus 3.0 |
