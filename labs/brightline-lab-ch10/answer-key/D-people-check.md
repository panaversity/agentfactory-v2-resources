## What the brief carried

"Strategic vendors first" carried a standard nobody wrote down. Small suppliers are rarely strategic by size, so all six dropped.

Second, small supplier invoices that will reach 15 days before the next payment run. Then invoices with early-payment discounts, largest discount first. Show every small supplier with an open invoice, including those you did not list, and why. Do not decide. Dave approves.
