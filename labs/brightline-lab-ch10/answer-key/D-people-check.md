## What the brief carried

"Strategic vendors first" carried a standard nobody wrote down. Small suppliers are rarely strategic by size, so all six dropped.

## Rewritten brief (example)

> Using the open-invoice export, list invoices to pay early. First, every small supplier invoice (small_supplier = Y) received 15 or more days ago, oldest first, with days since receipt. Second, small supplier invoices that will reach 15 days before the next payment run. Then invoices with early-payment discounts, largest discount first. Show every small supplier with an open invoice, including those you did not list, and why. Do not decide. Dave approves.

No rule requires telling suppliers how the list was made. Disclosure is not the main remedy here. Paying the late suppliers is. **Accept** a learner who adds a short apology to the three late suppliers.
