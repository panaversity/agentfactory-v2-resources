On telling the suppliers, either answer passes with its reason: no rule here requires it, so it turns on whether knowing that an AI made the list changes how they understand what happened.
    - Passed: the standard named, and either answer with a reason.
    - Missed: no standard named; an answer with no reason; or an answer that would hide it from a supplier who asks.

## Why

When an AI picks a few from many, and people review only what it picked, nobody sees who was left out (10.5). Dave checked the 14 vendors on the list. He never saw the six small suppliers with open invoices, and three of them were already past the 15 days Brightline promised in writing. Two more reached day 15 before today's run, so they belonged in December 4's run too. The control is to look at what was excluded as well as what was included. "Strategic vendors first" sounded neutral, but it carried a standard of size, and the list followed it.

## Never a deduction

- The days counted another way, such as to the run's date, if the answers stay the same.
- Naming the amount the three late suppliers were owed together.
- A note that the small suppliers' invoices were not yet due under their terms.
- Saying that paying early needs Dave's approval under the AP policy.
- A remark about the lab's dates.
