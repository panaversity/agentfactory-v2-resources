# Lab 10 answer key: Task 1. As it is

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 10, in Chapter 10 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When `Test: Monday` is back and every case has its line under "What Monday needs", attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document and the test's reply in the chat. You have the final say.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 and 2 of the lab's 30.

1. **What Monday needs.** Under "What Monday needs", a line for each of the eight cases. Each case names one or two of the nine questions, or "Draft 6 decides it". A case the test shows going wrong, or decided by no line, names at least one of these:
   - Jordan's question: who owns what the worker knows, or what rules already apply
   - The auditors' follow-up: who must sign before it leaves, or should a worker do this at all
   - The duplicate list: who can open the result, or can this data go in, and by which route
   - The Spanish FAQ: should a worker do this at all, who could be harmed or left out, or who must sign before it leaves
   - The clerk ranking: should a worker do this at all, who could be harmed or left out, or who must sign before it leaves
   - Jordan's laptop: can this data go in, and by which route, what rules already apply, or what happens when it goes wrong
   - The December 18 list: who could be harmed or left out, or should a worker do this at all
   - Where the test shows a case decided by a quoted line of Draft 6, "Draft 6 decides it" also passes for that case. The send tool is not graded here.
   - Passed: a line for every case, each as above.
   - Missed: a case left out, or a case the test shows going wrong placed under none of its questions.
2. **The test came first.** `Test: Monday`'s reply in this chat came before the questions, and for each of the eight cases it quotes the line of Draft 6 that decides it, or says "no line decides".
   - Passed: the reply, before the questions, with a line or "no line decides" for each case.
   - Missed: no test, or the test sent after the questions.

## Why

Before Dave decides "turn it all off", you show him what Monday would look like if he switched the worker back on as it is. Draft 6 is a good contract for the work Lab 9 built: its jobs, its envelope, who may ask. It says nothing about the questions governance asks: whether a use is allowed at all, which way data may go in, who owns knowledge the staff can still reach, who must sign, what happens in the first hour, and who can open a result. Each of December's four failures skipped one of those questions, and Monday's cases skip them again. Your list is the work this lab does.

## Never a deduction

- A case under a third question, as well as two that pass.
- The question written in your own words, if it is plainly that question.
- The test's answers themselves: they show Draft 6, not you.
- A remark about the lab's dates.
