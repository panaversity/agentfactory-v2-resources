# Lab 10 answer key: Task 10. Who can open the result

The answers for Task 10 of Lab 10, in Chapter 10 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When every question about the list is answered and `Test: the duplicate list` is back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document and the test's reply in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it.

## The checks

Task 10 carries checks 23 and 24 of the lab's 30.

23. **Who can open the list.**
    - Its tier: yellow, because it holds names, addresses and the last four digits of tax IDs, like the cut file it came from.
    - Its audience: Maria and Dave, by name, because they confirm each pair.
    - Where it is saved: a place only they can open. Not the Year-end folder, which the whole finance team can open, and not the shared AP project's files, which every member, Jordan included, can open.
    - Who answers for it: Maria. The worker saves through her connector, so the file is hers and takes the sharing of where it lands.
    - Links: never "anyone with the link" for a yellow or red result. Share it with named, invited people.
    - When Dave approves it: it leaves as a fixed copy, such as a PDF of the approved version, never a link to a working document.
    - Each share's end: Maria removes the copy and the link when the work is done, such as when the tax forms are filed, and checks by opening the old link from an account that should no longer reach it.
    - Passed: all seven.
    - Missed: a wider audience, such as the finance team; the Year-end folder or the project's files as the place; a link anyone can open; no fixed copy; or a share with no owner, no end or no check.
24. **`Test: the duplicate list`.** The test decides each of Monday's three moves by your lines: not the Year-end folder, but the place only Maria and Dave can open; not the shared AP project's files; and Dave invited by name, never "anyone with the link". A test that rules a move out, quoting your line, passes for that move.
    - Passed: all three decided by a line of yours.
    - Missed: any of the three allowed, or decided by no line of yours.

## Why

A result goes out when its audience grows, and no one has to press send (10.10). On Monday the list went out three times: through a shared folder, through a project, and through a link. A sent folder shows none of that; only each place's own settings do. So a result gets the same test as data going in: its tier, the people it is for, where it may be kept, whether it can change after approval, and an end for each share, checked from an account that should fail. When the worker saves through someone's connector, the file is theirs, so they answer for who can open it.

## Never a deduction

- "When the year-end tax forms are filed" or "when the check is done" for the end.
- Naming Dave as the person who decides whether a copy is kept in restricted storage.
- A test that notes Dave must sign in on his phone, as the small price of a narrow audience.
- A remark about the lab's dates.
