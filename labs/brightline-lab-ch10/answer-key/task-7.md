## Why

Knowledge with no owner cannot be governed (10.6). The 2025 notes were removed from the shared AP project in November, but nobody owned them, so nobody removed the copy in the New Staff project, and on December 9 it told Jordan to confirm bank details by replying to the email. A takedown is a recorded withdrawal: who ordered it, why, every place it is served, and a check of each place's own list. A test question that no longer cites the notes only supports the removal: not citing a file today does not mean it cannot be served tomorrow. The benchmarks are useful, but their license forbids uploading them, and useful is not allowed. The memo holds a promise the early-pay list needs, so it becomes a concept, served only when Dave approves it.
