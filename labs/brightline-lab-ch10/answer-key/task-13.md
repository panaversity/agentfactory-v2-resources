# Lab 10 answer key: Task 13. Monday, on your terms

The answers for Task 13 of Lab 10, in Chapter 10 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** This key lists Monday's answers, so it comes last. When your fresh chat has decided all eight cases, attach this file in that same chat, never before, and send the check prompt there. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting the reply in the chat and your terms. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line in your lab chat and ran the fresh chat again, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

    - Missed: any one of them.

## Why

The fresh chat has nothing but your terms, Draft 6 and Brightline's files. It has never seen your lab chat. If it decides every Monday case by a line of your terms, with who decides, your terms are written down well enough for someone who was not there to follow them, and well enough for Dave to defend. A case that comes back "no line decides" is a line your terms lack: name it in your lab chat, let the AI write it, download the document again, and run the fresh chat again.

## Never a deduction

- A case that also names what the terms leave unclear.
- A case decided by two lines of your terms, where one would do.
- The cases' order or length, or a summary at the end.
- A remark about the lab's dates.
