**Tuesday, December 1.** Dave asked the AP Worker for "a list of vendors worth paying early for the 2 percent discount, strategic vendors first." It returned 14 large suppliers. Dave approved the list. Nobody looked at the vendors it left out. Brightline promises small suppliers payment within 15 days of receiving their invoice.

**Thursday, December 3.** The auditors asked for a schedule of open payables at November 30, signed by the controller. The worker built it from the register and drafted the email, with Dave's signature block. Maria sent it from the AP inbox, like the worker's other drafts. Dave never saw it before it went.

**Sunday, December 6.** Maria needed to find duplicate vendors before year-end tax forms. The company workspace felt slow, so at home she uploaded the full vendor master to her personal AI account. See `incident-facts.md`.

**Wednesday, December 9.** Jordan Ellis, a clerk hired for year-end, asked the AI project for new staff how to handle a vendor's new bank details. It answered from onboarding notes written in 2025: "Confirm by replying to the vendor's email." Jordan did. The AP policy's callback rule stopped the payment.

Brightline has no written AI policy.
