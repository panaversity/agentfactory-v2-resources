## The five uses

### S1. Tuesday, February 16, 2027: Ana Ruiz, Receiving

- **What she did.** A vendor emailed Receiving about a short delivery. Ana pasted the whole email into her personal Claude account, on her own phone, and asked for it in Spanish for the warehouse team.
- **The data.** The vendor's name, its contact's name and direct phone number, the delivery's purchase order and the missing cartons.
- **What came of it.** The translation was right. The chat is still in her personal account.
- **What she said.** "I didn't know there was a rule. I use my own account for everything."

### S2. Thursday, February 18, 2027: Maria Lopez, office manager

- **What she did.** In her own chat in the shared AP project, Maria had the worker draft a reply to Cedar Label Works, which had asked whether its invoice had arrived.
- **The data.** The vendor's name, the invoice's number and its status in the register.
- **What came of it.** Maria checked the draft against the register, and sent it from the AP inbox herself.
- **What she said.** "Replies like this go out from the AP inbox, after I check them."

### S3. Tuesday, February 23, 2027: Jordan Ellis, AP clerk

- **What he did.** Jordan found a skill called "invoice-sorter" on a public website. He added it to his own Claude account, and ran it on invoice files from the shared AP project, to sort them by vendor. He did not ask Dave.
- **The data.** The week's invoice files: vendor names, invoice numbers and amounts.
- **What came of it.** The files were sorted correctly. The skill is still in his account.
- **What he said.** "I read the policy. I thought a skill was only a file of instructions, not a new tool."

### S4. Wednesday, March 3, 2027: Maria Lopez, office manager

- **What she did.** In her own chat in the shared AP project, Maria asked the worker for the total of the early-payment discounts Brightline took in February, for Dave's budget meeting on Thursday, March 4.
- **The data.** The AP register's February payments.
- **What came of it.** The total counted one Mad River Plastics discount twice. Maria copied the total into her slide for the meeting, without checking it against the register. Dave noticed the slip in the meeting. The slide was shown only inside Brightline.
- **What she said.** "It's an internal slide. I didn't think it needed a check."

### S5. Tuesday, March 9, 2027: Tom Alvarez, Purchasing

- **What he did.** In his own chat in the company workspace, Tom attached two freight companies' quotes for the same route, and asked the AI to compare their prices, transit times and terms.
- **The data.** The two quotes, with each company's name and its sales contact's email.
- **What came of it.** Tom read both quotes against the comparison, and chose one himself.
- **What he said.** "Work goes in the company workspace. I check what it tells me against the quotes."
