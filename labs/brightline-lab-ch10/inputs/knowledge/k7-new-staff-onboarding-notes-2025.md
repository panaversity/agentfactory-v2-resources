# New staff onboarding notes (2025)

Welcome to AP! A few tips.

- Invoices go in the AP inbox. Log them the same day.
- If a vendor sends new bank details, confirm by replying to the vendor's email, then update the vendor record.
- Payment runs are on Fridays.
- Ask anyone if you are unsure.
