# Warm-up: Riverside Sports Club

This warm-up has nothing to do with finance. It takes about 10 minutes. The club and its rules are invented.

## The AI summary

A new member asked an AI assistant to summarize the club's membership rules. The AI answered:

> You can cancel your membership at any time and get all of your money back. Anyone can join.

## The membership rules

1. **Joining.** Anyone aged 16 or over can join. A person under 16 can join only if a parent or guardian signs the form.
2. **Fees.** Members pay a joining fee of $40 once, and a monthly fee of $25 on the first day of each month.
3. **Cancelling.** A member can cancel at any time by giving 30 days' notice in writing. The membership ends 30 days after the club receives the notice.
4. **Refunds.** When a membership ends, the club returns the monthly fee for any full month that the member has paid for but not started. The joining fee is never returned.
5. **Guests.** A member can bring one guest a month at no cost.

## Your task

Run the four checkpoint questions on the AI summary.

1. What exactly is claimed?
2. What is it based on?
3. What is assumed?
4. What would change it?

Then check the rules. Write down each thing the summary got wrong, and the rule that shows it.
