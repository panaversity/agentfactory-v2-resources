# Treasury note: cash and borrowing, Q4 2026

Brightline Wholesale Supply, Columbus, Ohio. Prepared by the controller's office, October 1, 2026.

- Brightline keeps a minimum cash balance of $60,000 in its operating account. Any payment that would take the balance below that is funded from the line of credit.
- The line of credit costs 9% a year (annual percentage rate). Interest is charged only on the amount drawn.
- Brightline normally pays each invoice on its due date, in the Friday payment run. An invoice dated in September on net 30 terms is paid in October.
- Invoice INV-3115 from Hocking Equipment Leasing, for $148,500.00, is a one-time payment to buy warehouse equipment at the end of a lease. No similar payment is planned.
- In Q4, the operating balance is expected to sit between $55,000 and $80,000 on payment-run days, so some early payments will need the line of credit.
- Rule of thumb for AP: an early-payment discount is worth taking only when its annualized return is higher than the cost of the money used to pay early. Today that cost is 9% a year.
- How to annualize a discount offered as "d/n net N": discount rate divided by (1 minus discount rate), times 365, divided by (N minus n). Example: 2/10 net 30 is 0.02 / 0.98 x 365 / 20. Do this in a spreadsheet or calculator, not in a chat reply.
