# Lab 0.3 answer key: Task 1. The neat page, on both AI vendors

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 0.3, in Chapter 0.3 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your notes table has a column for each AI vendor and your answer under each, attach this file in your lab conversation, and send the lab's check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed against this key. Read the key yourself too: you have the final say.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 to 6 of the lab's 13.

1. **Both pages show their marks.** Passed when each pasted page has "## " in front of its headings and "- " in front of its items. Missed when a pasted page is drawn text with no marks.
2. **The rule is under Rules.** Passed when the $5,000 sentence is under Rules in both columns.
3. **The remark is under Remarks.** Passed when the $10,000 sentence is under Remarks in both columns, or your answer for a column names it as under the wrong heading.
4. **The gap is open.** Passed when the Canadian-dollar sentence is under Open questions in both columns, or your answer for a column names it as under the wrong heading.
5. **Both columns, and your answers.** Passed when every row has an entry for both AI vendors, and your answer to the column's question is recorded for each. "None" is an answer when it is true.
6. **Words the notes do not say.** Passed when your answer names each line a page added that the notes do not say, such as a sentence that the $5,000 limit "still applies", or says none and the page added none. Missed when a page added a line and your answer says none.

## Never a deduction

- Where the Friday sentence sits.
- The bank-details sentence under Rules.
- The two AI vendors putting a sentence in different places, or wording it differently.
- A page with more or fewer marks, such as bold words or a numbered step.
