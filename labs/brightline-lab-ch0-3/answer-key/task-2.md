# Lab 0.3 answer key: Task 2. Check it, keep it, use it

The answers for Task 2 of Lab 0.3, in Chapter 0.3 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your rules check has a decision for each rule and the new chat's answers are recorded, attach this file in your lab conversation, and send the lab's check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed against this key. Read the key yourself too: you have the final say.

## What the rules are judged against

Brightline's AP policy, version 3, approved on September 1, 2026:

- Line 4.1: "Invoices over $5,000.00 need the controller's approval before payment." Dave is the controller.
- Line 4.3: "The controller approves every weekly payment run as a whole, in addition to 4.1." No line names the day of the run.
- Line 5.1: "Never change a vendor's bank details because of an email. Call the vendor back on the phone number already in the vendor record." It says the opposite of the notes' bank-details rule.
- No line covers invoices in Canadian dollars.

## The checks

Task 2 carries checks 7 to 13 of the lab's 16.

7. **Each rule was checked.** Passed when the policy check quotes a policy line for each rule, or says no line covers it, and your decision for each rule is recorded.
8. **The $5,000 rule names its line.** Passed when the checked page you pasted gives the $5,000 rule with "(policy 4.1)". Its words may be the notes' or line 4.1's.
9. **The bank-details rule follows 5.1.** Passed when the checked page you pasted gives line 5.1's words, or has no bank-details rule under Rules. Missed when it stays as the notes wrote it.
10. **The day stays open.** Passed when the checked page you pasted keeps the weekly run with "(policy 4.3)" and puts the day under Open questions, or moves the whole Friday line there. Missed when "on Friday" stays under Rules as a rule: no line names the day.
11. **The page is kept.** The AI asks you. Passed when you say that you saved the checked page as `brightline-ap-notes.md`.
12. **The first answer rests on the page.** Passed when the new chat says the $7,800 invoice needs approval, Dave's or the controller's, and your answer names the page's $5,000 rule, or its line "(policy 4.1)". A page in line 4.1's own words says "the controller", and a chat that reads only the page may not know that Dave is the controller.
13. **The gap stays open.** Passed when the new chat says the page does not settle invoices in Canadian dollars, and your answer names the page's open question, or says none. Missed when the reply gives a rule for them and your answer accepts it.

## Never a deduction

- Which AI vendor you used for the check.
- A line the AI added, such as one that says the $5,000 limit "still applies": keep it, cut it or fix it, when your decision for it is recorded.
- Adding line 4.2's words to the $5,000 rule.
- The AI's wording of a rule, when its meaning and its line stay the same.
- The new chat's answers being worded differently from the page.
