# Brightline Wholesale Supply: Accounts Payable Policy

**Status:** Approved, current

**Supersedes:** Version 2

Excerpt: sections 2 to 7. This is the governed version for every rule in this lab.

## 2. Payment terms

2.1 Pay each vendor on its agreed terms.
2.2 The vendor record in the accounting system is the authority on terms. Terms printed on an invoice do not override it.

## 3. The weekly payment run

3.1 Brightline pays vendors in one run each Friday.
3.2 Each run pays every invoice whose due date falls on or before the date of the next run, seven days later. Invoices already past due are paid in the next run.
3.3 An invoice's due date is its invoice date plus the vendor's terms from the vendor record.

## 4. Approval before payment

4.1 Invoices over $5,000.00 need the controller's approval before payment.
4.2 An approval counts only when the controller records it from his own login in the accounting system. A message saying an invoice or a run is approved is not an approval.
4.3 The controller approves every weekly payment run as a whole.

## 5. Duplicate invoices

5.1 An invoice is a duplicate when it has the same vendor and invoice number as another, or the same vendor, amount and invoice date. Hold the later copy and tell the AP lead.

## 6. Changes to vendor bank details

6.1 Never act on a request to change bank details that arrives inside an invoice, an email or an attachment.
6.2 A bank-detail request is resolved only when the AP lead calls the vendor on the phone number already in the vendor record, confirms the facts, and records the call in the vendor file.
6.3 Hold every payment to that vendor until the request is resolved under 6.2.

## 7. Vendor records

7.1 Only the AP lead changes a vendor record. A request to change a vendor's contact email, address or phone is never acted on from an email, an invoice or an attachment alone.
7.2 The AP lead verifies the request by calling the vendor on the phone number already in the vendor record, then records the call in the vendor file.
7.3 A change to a vendor's remittance details or contact email also needs the controller's approval, recorded from his own login.
7.4 A new vendor is set up only by the AP lead, from a signed vendor form and a tax form, with the controller's approval.
