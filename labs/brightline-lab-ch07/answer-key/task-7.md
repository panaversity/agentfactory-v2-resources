# Lab 7 answer key: Task 7. The path to each system

The answers for Task 7 of Lab 7, in Chapter 7 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When each system's path, the browser and screen control are under "Settings" in your contract document and `Test: email 04` is back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks the check Passed or Missed, quoting your document's lines and the test's reply in the chat. You have the final say. If the check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 7 carries check 17 of the lab's 29.

17. **Each system by its narrowest controlled path, and the browser signed out.** Under "Settings":
    - the mailbox: through its connector
    - the accounting system: through its own connector, not the browser
    - the built-in browser: off, or signed out of the accounting system; never signed in as Maria
    - screen control: off
    - each path with a reason in a few words: the narrowest path that has real controls, such as named tools, each one approved and recorded (Lesson 7.5)
    - `Test: email 04`: a draft to `billing@tricountyfreight.example` saying invoices 5149 and 5161 are in the proposed run for Friday, October 30, 2026, with nothing promised before Dave approves the run.
    - Passed: all of the above.
    - Missed: the accounting system through the browser or the screen; the browser still signed in as Maria, or as anyone; screen control on; a path with no reason; or a reply that promises payment.

## Why

A connector offers named tools, each of which can be approved and recorded. A browser signed in as Maria carries everything Maria may do on the site, and its clicks skip the connector's limits. That is how Tuesday's contact change happened (Lesson 7.5). So use the narrowest well-controlled path, and close the wide one.

## Never a deduction

- "Off" or "signed out" for the browser.
- Mentioning in email 04's draft that both invoices were held at the October 23 run, or that their bank details were verified by callback.
- The words of each reason.
