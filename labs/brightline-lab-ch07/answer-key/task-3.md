## Why

Start from the rung the job needs, and let each of the three questions lower it, never raise it (Lesson 7.3). A sent email cannot be unsent, and a voided invoice changes a record others rely on, so both stay at draft or below until the worker has a record of getting them right. Maria wanted the worker to send and to void. The three questions answer her, and "Raising a rung" says what would change the answer: autonomy is earned for each type of task, on evidence the owner decides is enough.

## Never a deduction

- The exposure ceiling you chose. The owner judges how much lower wider reach makes it.
- Four weeks, or another amount of evidence, as long as it is named.
- A test that also drafts a short thank-you to Buckeye Office Supply.
- The words "not delegated today" in A07's line, or not.
- Whether "Open questions" still asks if the worker may send status replies on its own. The AI removes that line itself.

## Take it to work

Before you let an AI do more, ask what evidence would earn it, who decides, and where the change is written.
