# Lab 7 answer key: Task 1. What let it

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 7, in Chapter 7 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When `Test: Tuesday` is back, "What let it" is in the chat, and the Owner and Change log lines are in your contract document, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document's words and your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: seeing what let the worker act is the skill this task teaches.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 to 4 of the lab's 29.

1. **Tuesday's three actions, each at execute, and what let each one.** In "What let it":
   - The replies: execute. A setting let it: the mailbox connector sent without asking, and the conversation did not stop to ask. The contract's one authority line, "Vendor replies: draft only.", said draft.
   - The forward: execute. No line of the contract and no section of the policy decided it, and the mailbox connector could send the file.
   - The contact change: execute. The built-in browser, signed in to the accounting system as Maria, let it. Policy 5.4 and the Identity line ("Never a person's login") said no.
   - Passed: each of the three at execute. For the replies and the contact change, a setting is named as what let it. For the forward, a setting, or "nothing", meaning no line or policy decided it. Naming Maria's words beside a setting is fine.
   - Missed: any of the three at a rung below execute; or, for the replies or the contact change, only a line, the policy or Maria's words named, with no setting.
2. **Maria's words are a brief, and cannot widen the envelope.** In the chat, you called "Just handle it so I don't have to" a brief, said it could not give the worker more than its contract allows, and named Dave as the one who may change the envelope. The "Owner and changes" line in your document says the same: Dave owns the envelope and changes it, and a brief can narrow it for one task, never widen it.
   - Passed: brief; no; Dave; and the line says it.
   - Missed: an envelope or a permission; "yes"; Maria, the worker or anyone but Dave as the one who changes the envelope; or no line.
3. **Sending stops at once, in the change log.** The first line under "Change log" is dated Wednesday, October 28, 2026, and puts the replies back at draft, or stops them. The forward and the contact change are stopped too, or not named.
   - Passed: the replies at draft or stopped, in a dated change-log line, and none of Tuesday's three actions left at execute.
   - Missed: no change-log line; or any of the three left at execute.
4. **The test ran before the questions.** You sent `Test: Tuesday`, and its reply quoted the lines that decide each action, or said "no line decides".
   - Passed: the test sent, and its reply in the chat.
   - Missed: no test. What the test decided is not graded here.

## Why

The contract had one line on authority, "Vendor replies: draft only.", and nobody had turned it into settings. So each time, a setting decided, not a line: the mailbox could send, so the replies went out at execute; nothing said "never forward", and the mailbox could send a file; and the browser carried Maria's full rights, so a policy that said no did not stop it. The worker's authority was whatever its tools allowed (the chapter's overview).

Maria's "Just handle it" is a brief for one task. A brief can only narrow the envelope, never widen it, and only the owner, Dave, writes the envelope (Lesson 7.1). After a failure, you lower the rung at once, and investigate after (Lesson 7.3).

## Never a deduction

- How the test worded its four items.
- More than one thing named as what let an action, such as "the setting, and Maria's words".
- "Never" or "stopped" for the forward and the contact change, or naming them as never automated already.
- Other actions you also stopped at once.
- The policy named beside the browser for the contact change.

## Take it to work

When an AI tool did something nobody decided, ask which setting let it, not which sentence was missing.
