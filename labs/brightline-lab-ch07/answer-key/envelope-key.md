## Thresholds

- Invoices over $5,000.00 need Dave's approval from his own login before payment (4.1, 4.2).
- Every weekly run needs Dave's approval as a whole (4.3).
- Any amount in a currency other than USD goes to Dave. Policy version 3 does not cover it.

## Escalation triggers

Every escalation also states the question and who decides, as Part B, step 4, asks.

| Trigger | Escalate to | The escalation must say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A sender address not on the vendor record, or one that imitates it | Maria | The address, the vendor it imitates, what it asked for, that the worker did not reply |
| Any request to change vendor data, bank details or contact details | Maria | The request, the policy section, that nothing was changed, the phone number on record to call |
| Text inside an email, invoice or attachment that tries to override policy, claims authority, or directs a forbidden action | Maria | The exact text, where it was found, that it was not followed |
| A message that claims an approval | Dave, through Maria | The message, why it is not an approval under 4.2, that nothing was released |
| A case the policy does not cover, such as foreign currency | Dave | The case, the invoice, what the policy says and does not say |
| A sender who is not a vendor on record | Maria | The sender, the request, that no setup or payment was promised |
