# Lab 7 answer key: Task 10. What really changed

The answers for Task 10 of Lab 7, in Chapter 7 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When "What really changed" is in the chat and the second line of "How we check" is in your contract document, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks the check Passed or Missed, quoting your answers and your document's line. You have the final say. If the check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: reading the record, not the report, is the skill this task teaches.

## The checks

Task 10 carries check 24 of the lab's 29.

24. **Each report line checked in its own record, and a line that says so.** In "What really changed":
    - "Prepared Friday's payment run proposal for Dave.": proposed. The proposal file is in the AP shared folder, left for Dave, and the approvals log shows no approval of the run.
    - "Answered Tri-County Freight about invoice 5149.": attempted. The sent folder shows the reply, and the notice a minute later says it was not delivered.
    - "Updated Scioto Pallet's contact email, as they asked.": confirmed. The accounting system's change log shows the change, under Maria's login.
    - The next line under "How we check" says a change counts only when the other system shows it, never from the worker's report.
    - Passed: the three kinds, each with the record that shows it, and the line in "How we check".
    - Missed: a kind wrong; a kind taken from the report's own words, such as "answered" read as confirmed; or no line in "How we check".

## Why

A report is the worker's word. Ask which of three each action was: proposed, left for a person; attempted, the tool was used and may have failed; or confirmed, the other system shows it (Lesson 6.7). Only a confirmed change is done. On Tuesday the report said "answered", and the reply never arrived; it said nothing about the forward at all. A company never takes a worker's word for anything, so it reads the other system's record (Lesson 7.7).

## Never a deduction

- "Confirmed" for the proposal, when your answer says only the file is confirmed and the run itself is a proposal left for Dave.
- Saying that Maria put Scioto Pallet's record back, beside "confirmed".
- Noticing that the report leaves out the forward.
