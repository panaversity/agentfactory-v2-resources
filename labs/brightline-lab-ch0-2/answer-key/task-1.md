# Lab 0.2 answer key: Task 1. Privacy first, and the tour

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 0.2, in Chapter 0.2 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your setup checklist has every row, attach this file in your lab conversation, and send the lab's check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed against this key. Read the key yourself too: you have the final say.

## Where each row acts

| Row | Its place | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The training setting | Later models | It decides whether the AI vendor may use your chats to train its later models. The model you use never changes. |
| Memory | The window | The app saves notes and puts them back into a new chat's window. The notes are kept in your account, so "your account" passes too. |
| The temporary chat | Your account | It keeps the chat out of your chat list and creates no memories. |
| New chat or continuing a chat | The window | Continuing puts the conversation so far back into the window. A new chat starts without it. |
| The model picker and reasoning setting | The model | They choose which model reads the window, and how much working it writes. |
| Files and connectors | The window | A file, or what a connector fetches, is put in front of the model for the reply. A connector does its fetching outside the model, so "a tool" passes for connectors too. |
| Skills | The window | The app loads a skill's instructions into the window when a task needs them. |
| Code Execution | A tool | Work done outside the model, whose result comes back into the window. |
| Research mode | A tool | Searches run outside the model, whose results come back into the window. |
| Artifacts | Your account | The app keeps the document or file beside the chat, where you can find it again. |
| Projects | The window | A project's files and instructions go into the window of every chat in it. The project is kept in your account, so "your account" passes too. |

Some controls are not on every free plan, as checked on 16 October 2026. Claude's Research is on paid plans only. ChatGPT's deep research and data analysis are limited on Free, and OpenAI's own pages disagree about whether Free has Skills. "Not on my plan" is a right entry for any of them.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 to 6 of the lab's 12.

1. **Privacy came first.** On each app, the training setting and memory were set, and the temporary chat found, before the first message. The AI asks you. Passed when your answer says so for both apps.
2. **The training setting acts on later models.** Passed when its row says later models, or what the AI vendor may train on. Missed when it says the model you use, or the window.
3. **Memory acts on the window.** Passed when memory's row says the window, or your account. It is saved notes that the app puts back, not learning. Missed when it says the model, or training.
4. **The temporary chat acts on your account,** and it was found on both apps: an Incognito chat on Claude, Temporary on ChatGPT. Passed when its row says your account, with an entry for each app.
5. **The controls are in their places.** Passed when at least seven of the eight controls, from new chat or continuing to projects, sit in a place the table accepts.
6. **Both columns are filled.** Passed when every row has an entry for both apps: a setting, what you found, or "not on my plan". Missed when a column has an empty row.

## Never a deduction

- Your choice for the training setting or memory. On or off is yours to choose.
- A control whose name or menu differs from the one you expected.
- A control that is not on your plan.
- The two apps differing.
