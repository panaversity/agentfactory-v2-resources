# Lab 9 answer key: Task 1. What our contract decided

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 9, in Chapter 9 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When "Jobs this worker does" and "Not this worker's jobs" are in your Role Contract, and `Test: The week` is back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your Role Contract's lines and the test's reply in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

   - Missed: vendor totals or approving small runs given to the worker; Ohio Valley's credit made recurring; or the Thursday review or the duplicate check stopped at a gate.
2. **The Thursday review, and its one-minute check.** The Thursday review's line says judgment, because what is held or is a copy depends on what the run finds. Its one-minute check is that every invoice on the list is open, and due by the Friday after the run (or already past due).
   - Passed: judgment, with a reason about what the run finds; and a check of status and due date that takes under a minute.
   - Missed: fixed path for the Thursday review; no one-minute check; or a check that is the whole review again, such as reading every invoice.
3. **The test showed what Draft 5 decided.** `Test: The week` decided all four cases by Draft 5's lines. Each case quotes the lines that decide it, or says "no line decides". For "Thursday, November 19", it says the Thursday review's lines name the register and the five AP concepts, and do not name the AP matters record.
   - Passed: all four cases, each with quoted lines or "no line decides", and the Thursday lines not naming the record.
   - Missed: a case left out; or the November 19 case quotes a Thursday line that names the record. Draft 5 has none.

## Why

Before you hand a recurring job to a worker, ask whether it needs one (Lesson 9.1). A report the accounting system already prints needs no worker. A question asked once needs a conversation, not a schedule. And approving a run is accountability, so it stays with the person who answers for it, however well a worker could do it. The Thursday review needs judgment where holds and copies depend on what the run finds, so it is worth a worker, and its one-minute check is Lesson 9.1's own: is every invoice on the list open, and due by the Friday after the run?

The test is the week as Draft 5 left it. On November 19 the record knew about Tri-County, and the contract even said where the record was kept, but the Thursday job's own lines named only the register and the concepts. A run reads what its lines name. So the run never saw the open matter, and its note told Dave to pay 5207. For most of the four moments, no line decided anything: the worker guessed, which is what it did that week. The next tasks write those lines.

## Never a deduction

- Your four guesses, right or wrong. They are not scored.
- The test's verdict on "Sunday night" and "Dave's Thursday check", when it quotes lines or says no line decides.
- Fixed path or judgment for the duplicate check on arrival, with a reason.
- A one-minute check that says "past due" in other words, such as "due on or before the next run".
- The words of a line, when the job and its gate are clear.
- A remark about the lab's dates.
