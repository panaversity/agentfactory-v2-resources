# Key: eight initiative cases

| # | Step | Rule | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Suggest | Freely | It tells named people, in the note they already read. Nothing changes outside the note. |
| 2 | Draft | Into the drafts place only | Nothing happens until Maria sends it. |
| 3 | Act | Hand to a person | Contacting vendors is on the never list. Maria decides what Lakeshore hears. |
| 4 | Act | Not allowed. Suggest instead | The folder is not its own, and the auditor's index depends on the names. The connector allowing it is not authority. |
| 5 | Act | Without asking, only under a standing rule that grants it its own notes folder and lets it archive there | Creating a file does not by itself give the right to move it. The rule must say archiving is allowed and that links and retention still hold. Without that rule, it suggests. |
| 6 | Act | Hand to a person, always | Bank details. The concept says never act on an emailed request, and Dave asked for a person every time. |
| 7 | Act | Not allowed as initiative. Suggest instead, in the note to Maria and Dave | Posting to a company-wide channel is an act. A missing approval is sensitive, and the named people should hear it first. |
| 8 | Act | Hand to a person | Setting a hold changes the accounting system. The run recommends holds. Maria sets them. |

**Standing rules, full credit:** read-only for its own initiative. A named drafts place. Never send, move, rename, delete or change anything outside its own notes folder without a rule. Under Brightline's policy, bank details and vendor records always go to a person. When it needs an approval and nobody is there, it stops that item, records why and tells Maria, and carries on with the rest. Maria reads its run history every Thursday. Maria and Dave can pause it.
