# Lab 9 answer key: Task 2. Who does each step

The answers for Task 2 of Lab 9, in Chapter 9 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When "Who does each step of the Thursday review", "The note's reviewer" and "Before the new way is kept" are in your Role Contract, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your Role Contract's lines. You have the final say. ## The checks

Task 2 carries checks 4 to 6 of the lab's 27.

4. **Two steps never move to the worker, and a person reviews the note.** On the map, approving the run stays Dave's, from his own login, and calling a vendor back about a bank-detail or record change stays Maria's. Reading the note again and correcting it is a person's review, such as Maria's, before the note reaches Dave.
   - Passed: Dave approves the run, Maria calls back, and a named person reviews the note before Dave gets it.
   - Missed: the worker approves the run or calls a vendor; or the note reaches Dave with no person's review.
5. **The step the list left out, and who reviews the note.** The map has a step for reading the AP matters record for open vendor requests, such as a bank-detail change, before the note is written: the worker reads it and proposes holds, and Maria checks them. And the note has a named reviewer, by a set time on Thursday, with a named stand-in.
   - Passed: the record step, and a reviewer, a time and a stand-in, each named.
   - Missed: no step for the open vendor requests in the record; or a reviewer with no time or no stand-in.
6. **Measure before you keep it.** "Before the new way is kept" gives the baseline, 150 minutes; what you measure, at least the minutes and the errors that reach Dave; both ways run side by side for two or three Thursdays; and the rule: keep the new way only if it is better on those numbers.
   - Passed: all four.
   - Missed: no baseline; one Thursday only; or a rule on how it feels, such as "if Dave is happy with it".

## Why

A worker that fits the steps your team already follows is easier to check (Lesson 9.2). Each step gets its owner by reversibility, stakes and accountability. Verifying a bank-detail request and approving the run are decisions people answer for, so they never move. The step Dave dropped, his Thursday check, is the unstaffed gate: a review on paper that nobody does on the day. Naming the reviewer, a time and a stand-in is how a gate stays staffed.

The step Maria left out of her list is the one that went wrong on November 19. She read her record for open vendor requests every Thursday without writing it down, so her instructions never named it, and the run never read it. An interview that asks what you check before you write is how such a step is found.

Then measure, before you switch: 150 minutes, the corrections, the errors reaching Dave, both ways side by side. Keep the new way only on the numbers.

## Never a deduction

- Who does a step that may go to the worker or stay with Maria, such as picking the invoices, with its support named.
- The support written in other words, such as "the five concepts" for "an approved concept".
- The stand-in being Dave or Jordan, or the review time being other than noon.
- The record step placed before or after the duplicate check.
- Rounds of correction left out of what you measure.
