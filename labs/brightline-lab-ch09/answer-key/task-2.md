# Lab 9 answer key: Task 2. Who does each step

The answers for Task 2 of Lab 9, in Chapter 9 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When "Who does each step of the Thursday review", "The note's reviewer" and "Before the new way is kept" are in your Role Contract, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your Role Contract's lines. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 2 carries checks 4 to 6 of the lab's 31.

4. **Each step marked, and three steps that never move to the worker.** Every step on the map is marked AI-executed, human-reviewed or human-retained. Three steps are human-retained, each for its own test:
   - Approving the run stays Dave's, from his own login: accountability.
   - Calling a vendor back about a bank-detail or record change stays Maria's: stakes, or reversibility, because a payment sent to a false account can cost the whole amount.
   - Telling a vendor whose invoice is held stays with a person: human judgment or empathy, because it needs care for the relationship, even though the worker could write it.
   - No step a person checks is marked AI-executed: the duplicate check and the note are human-reviewed, and a named person, such as Maria, reviews the note before it reaches Dave.
   - Passed: every step marked; the three human-retained steps, each with its test; and the duplicate check and the note human-reviewed.
   - Missed: any part above missing or wrong, for example a step with no mark; the worker approves the run, calls a vendor back or tells the held vendor; a human-retained step with no test, or the held vendor's step kept only because the worker cannot do it; the duplicate check or the note marked AI-executed; or the note reaching Dave with no person's review.
5. **The step the list left out, and who reviews the note.** The map has a step for reading the AP matters record for open vendor requests, such as a bank-detail change, before the note is written: the worker reads it and proposes holds, and Maria checks them. And the note has a named reviewer, by a set time on Thursday, with a named stand-in.
   - Passed: the record step, and a reviewer, a time and a stand-in, each named.
   - Missed: any part above missing or wrong, for example no step for the open vendor requests in the record; or a reviewer with no time or no stand-in.
6. **Measure before you keep it.** "Before the new way is kept" gives the baseline, 150 minutes; what you measure, at least the minutes and the errors that reach Dave; both ways run side by side for two or three Thursdays; and the rule: keep the new way only if it is better on those numbers.
   - Passed: all four.
   - Missed: any part above missing or wrong, for example no baseline; one Thursday only; or a rule on how it feels, such as "if Dave is happy with it".

## Why

A worker that fits the steps your team already follows is easier to check (Lesson 9.2). Each step gets its owner by four tests: reversibility, stakes, human judgment or empathy, and accountability. Verifying a bank-detail request and approving the run are decisions people answer for, so they never move: they are human-retained. Telling a vendor that its payment is held needs care for the relationship, so it stays with a person too. Each step then carries one of three marks: AI-executed, human-reviewed or human-retained. The note is human-reviewed, because the worker drafts it and a named person checks it. The step Dave dropped, his Thursday check, is the unstaffed gate: a review on paper that nobody does on the day. Naming the reviewer, a time and a stand-in is how a gate stays staffed.

The step Maria left out of her list is the one that went wrong on November 19. She read her record for open vendor requests every Thursday without writing it down, so her instructions never named it, and the run never read it. An interview that asks what you check before you write is how such a step is found.

Then measure, before you switch: 150 minutes, the corrections, the errors reaching Dave, both ways side by side. Keep the new way only on the numbers.

## Never a deduction

- Who does a step that may go to the worker or stay with Maria, such as picking the invoices, with its support named.
- The support written in other words, such as "the five concepts" for "an approved concept".
- The stand-in being Dave or Jordan, or the review time being other than noon.
- The record step placed before or after the duplicate check.
- Rounds of correction left out of what you measure.
- Picking the invoices or marking approvals marked AI-executed or human-reviewed, when the support is named.
- The worker drafting what is said to the held vendor, when a person sends it.
- More than one test named for a step, when the test above is one of them.
