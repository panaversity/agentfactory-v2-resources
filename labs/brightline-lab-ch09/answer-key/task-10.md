# Lab 9 answer key: Task 10. One page for Dave

The answers for Task 10 of Lab 9, in Chapter 9 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When "Note to Dave" holds parts 1 to 6, its limits and the lines for Linda, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting "Note to Dave" and your Role Contract. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a sentence, you still get it: the words are the skill this task teaches.

## The checks

Task 10 carries checks 23 and 24 of the lab's 31.

23. **The facts, from the contract.** Parts 1 to 3 say what runs and when, both jobs; what it reads, the inputs the standing specs name; and what it never does, as the contract's lines say, such as approving or paying.
    - Passed: all three, each matching the Role Contract, and nothing the contract does not say.
    - Missed: any part above missing or wrong, for example a part that says something the contract does not, such as a time or an input it does not name; or a part missing.
24. **Your own words.**
    - Part 4 names who still checks, by name: the note's reviewer, and Dave approving the run from his own login.
    - Part 5 says how we will know it failed: the check outside the run, at a set time, by a named person.
    - Part 6 gives only measured numbers: the 150-minute baseline, and that the new way is not measured yet, because the two ways run side by side only from Thursday, December 3.
    - The limits say what it does reliably, such as applying the same rules to every invoice each week, and name at least one thing it cannot do: it can miss what it was never given; it can write a well-organized note that is wrong; it runs on Thanksgiving if the schedule says Thursday.
    - The note never says "by itself", "fully automated" or "hands-free".
    - Nothing undersells it: the note never says it "only drafts", and Part 4 has a person review the note, not redo the review by hand.
    - "For Linda:" says what the AI does in both jobs, the Thursday note and the check of each invoice email, in words someone new to AI can follow, explaining or avoiding terms such as "run", "brief" or "AP Worker", and says that Dave still approves every payment.
    - Passed: all seven.
    - Missed: any part above missing or wrong, for example no name for who checks; a check the run does on itself; a figure for the new way, such as minutes saved, before any side-by-side week; no limit, or nothing it does reliably; one of the three phrases anywhere in the note; "it only drafts", or Maria doing the whole review by hand again; or a line for Linda that uses an AI term she must guess at, leaves out a job, or leaves out who decides.

## Why

The design works only if the people around it keep doing their part, and how you describe it decides whether they do (Lesson 9.7). "By itself" made Dave drop the check the design still needed. So the note names the human gate instead: who still checks, by name, and how anyone would know it failed. It claims only numbers you measured. The two ways run side by side only from December 3, so today there is the 150-minute baseline, and nothing measured yet for the new way, and the note says so. And it says the limits plainly, so the design keeps working when you are no longer there to explain it. It does not undersell the worker either: a reader told "it only drafts" may have the whole review done by hand again. Linda has never used AI at work, so she cannot fill in what the note leaves out. Her lines use no word she would have to guess at.

## Never a deduction

- Sentences longer or shorter than one line.
- Part 4 naming Dave's approval of the run, and not the invoices that need it.
- Part 5 naming what the checker does if the note is missing.
- Part 6 saying when the numbers will come, such as after three side-by-side Thursdays.
- More than one limit, or limits in your own words.
- The note's first line, and its date.
- Parts 1 to 3 with or without the contract's lines named.
- The lines for Linda naming Maria's review too.
