## Why

The design is the same on either AI vendor (Lesson 9.5). The products differ in three ways that change your setup: whose name the work runs under, what can start it, and how its initiative is limited. A ChatGPT team task runs under the team's service account. Anthropic's Cowork pages do not say whose name a scheduled task in Claude Cowork carries, and a scheduled task in Claude Code acts as you, so you check before you rely on it. ChatGPT documents tasks that start from Gmail; Anthropic documents no email trigger, so on Claude the email check is a gap with an owner. And on a dot, Pause does not stop scheduled runs, so you write your own initiative rules and limit what the worker can reach.
