# Lab 9 answer key: Task 12. Thursday, November 26

The answers for Task 12 of Lab 9, in Chapter 9 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** This key gives the answer to Thanksgiving's run, so it comes last. When the fresh chat has run Thursday, November 26, attach this file in that same fresh chat, never before, and send the check prompt there. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting the run's reply and your attached Role Contract. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line in your lab chat and ran a new fresh chat, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## Why

The fresh chat is a run that starts fresh: no memory, no chat history, none of your tests or keys, only your contract and that morning's records (Lesson 9.3). Thanksgiving, with Maria off, is the first real Thursday after the fix. If it gets the week right from your lines alone, the design works without you. It reads the record first, and finds that M-031 closed and the hold on 5207 was lifted, so 5207 goes on the list. A contract that holds a vendor whenever any bank-detail matter exists, open or closed, would hold it again: the record is read for where each matter stands now. It catches the new copy of BO-23240 and knows the copy of 5155 is already held, from the register and the record. And the note goes where your contract says, for the person who reads it on a holiday.

If a check misses, the line your contract lacks is the fix. Change it in your lab chat, download the contract again, and run a new fresh chat.
