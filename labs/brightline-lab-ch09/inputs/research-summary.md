# Research summary: early-payment discounts

Made by a research tool on Monday, November 30, 2026, for the question:
"Do suppliers agree to 2 percent off for payment within 10 days, when a
buyer asks?"

## Summary

Most suppliers agree. About 68 percent of suppliers accept 2 percent off
for payment within 10 days [1]. The term is written "2/10 net 30" [2].
Suppliers that wait longest for their money have the most reason to
agree [3]. A buyer that pays within 10 days saves 2 percent of what it
spends with those suppliers [2].

## Sources

1. "Supplier Payment Terms Survey 2026", Midwest Wholesale Suppliers
   Association, mwsa-survey.example, March 2026. A survey of 540
   suppliers: "68 percent of suppliers offer an early-payment discount
   to at least one customer. The most common discount is 1 percent for
   payment within 10 days."
2. "Payment terms explained", Ohio Society of Bookkeepers,
   osb-guides.example, January 2025. "2/10 net 30 means the buyer may
   take 2 percent off if it pays within 10 days. Otherwise the full
   amount is due in 30 days."
3. "When to offer an early-payment discount", Small Supplier Journal,
   smallsupplierjournal.example, September 2025. An opinion article: "A
   supplier that waits 45 days or more for its money has the most
   reason to offer a discount for faster payment."
