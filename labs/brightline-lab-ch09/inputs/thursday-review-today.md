# The Thursday pre-run review, as Maria does it today, in her own chat with the AP Worker

Measured by Maria over the five Thursdays in October 2026. Average 150 minutes. Errors still reached Dave: on Thursday, October 22, he found seven problems in the run package before he approved the run (Chapter 6).

| # | Step | Who | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Export the open invoices from the accounting system | Maria | 10 |
| 2 | Pick the invoices due by next Friday, plus anything past due | Maria | 25 |
| 3 | Mark which ones need Dave's approval | Maria | 15 |
| 4 | Check for duplicates against earlier invoices | Maria | 30 |
| 5 | Check her SSoR record for open vendor requests, and hold what policy says to hold | Maria | 20 |
| 6 | Write the pre-run note: the run list, totals, approvals needed, holds, duplicates, questions | Maria | 35 |
| 7 | Read the note again and correct it | Maria | 15 |
| 8 | Send it to Dave | Maria | 0 |
| 9 | Approve the run from his own login, on Thursday afternoon | Dave | not counted |
| 10 | Call a vendor back about a bank-detail or record change | Maria | as needed |

Dave used to read the run list himself every Thursday afternoon, before he approved the run. He stopped after Maria's email of November 9.
