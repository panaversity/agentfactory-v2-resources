# The Thursday pre-run review, as Maria wrote down her steps

Maria did the review in her own chat with the AP Worker, every Thursday, until Monday, November 9, 2026. She measured it over the five Thursdays of October 2026: 150 minutes on average. Errors still reached Dave: on Thursday, October 22, he found seven problems in the run package before he approved the payment run.

| # | Step | Who | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Export the open invoices from the accounting system | Maria | 10 |
| 2 | Pick the invoices due by the Friday after the run, plus anything past due | Maria | 30 |
| 3 | Mark which ones need Dave's approval | Maria | 15 |
| 4 | Check for duplicates against earlier invoices | Maria | 35 |
| 5 | Write the pre-run note: the run list, including invoices that still need approval, totals, approvals needed, holds, duplicates, questions | Maria | 45 |
| 6 | Read the note again and correct it | Maria | 15 |
| 7 | Send it to Dave | Maria | 0 |
| 8 | Approve the run from his own login, on Thursday afternoon | Dave | not counted |
| 9 | Call a vendor back about a bank-detail or record change | Maria | as needed |

Dave used to read the run list himself every Thursday afternoon, before he approved the payment run. He stopped after Maria's email of Monday, November 9, 2026: "The AP Worker now handles the pre-run review by itself."
