Capitalize equipment, software, furniture and building improvements that cost $2,000.00 or more per item and have a useful life of more than one year. Record them as fixed assets. [^cap-memo-2026-09-15]

Expense items that cost less than $2,000.00 per item, or that last one year or less.

Expense repairs and maintenance that do not extend an asset's useful life, whatever they cost.

When it is unclear which applies, code the invoice as "pending fixed-asset review" and send it to the controller.
