# Review Contract

**Task:** (one line, from the brief's outcome)
**Reviewer:** (the person who signs)
**Written:** (the date and time you write it, before you open anything in worker-output/)

## 1. What must be checked
The facts, figures and decisions that decide whether the work is usable.

## 2. What evidence comes back
What the worker must return so you can check each item above without redoing the work.

## 3. What counts as success
Tests the output must pass. Each one should be something a second reviewer could run and get the same answer. Include an identity check: every source ID appears exactly once, with none missing, repeated or unexpected.

## 4. What stops the worker
Conditions under which the worker must stop and ask instead of continuing.

## Share with the worker
Share the whole contract. Mark the lines the brief must carry, at least the evidence to return and the stop conditions. Verification and the verdict stay with you.
