# Lab 6 answer key: Task 12. Research on your own work

The answers for Task 12 of Lab 6, in Chapter 6 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the AI has said "Task 12 is done", attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks the check Passed or Missed, quoting your answers in the chat. You have the final say.

## The check

Task 12 carries check 31, and it is unscored: the lab's 30 scored checks are in Tasks 1 to 11. It grades the steps you took, never the search result, which changes from day to day.

31. **The four source checks, taken.** In your lab chat:
    - you opened the source behind the main claim, and said whether it says what the claim says
    - you named the pages that copy one source, counted as one, or said none do
    - you looked at each source's date, and said whether any is too old for your question
    - you asked for evidence that disagrees with the main claim
    - Passed: all four steps.
    - Missed: any step not taken.

## Ask yourself

- Did the main claim hold up, and what would you tell someone who acts on it?
- Which source would you trust most: an official one, a recent one, or one the others copy?
- Where good sources disagree, did the answer say so, or pick a side without saying why?

## Never a deduction

- The question you chose, the sources found, and whether the main claim held up.
- Skipping this task when your app has no web search.

## Take it to work

Before you act on research, open the source behind the claim your decision rests on.
