# One good Review Contract

**Task:** proposed payment run for Friday, October 30, 2026.
**Reviewer:** Dave Kowalski, Controller.
**Written:** Thursday, October 29, 2026, 2:00 p.m., before opening the package. In normal work this contract would be agreed on Monday, before Maria briefed the worker.

## 1. What must be checked
- Every open invoice has exactly one action, and the action follows policy version 3.
- Every amount, due date, approval and payment term matches the source files.
- The totals I sign: pay now, pay after approval, and the run total.
- Every policy section and approval ID cited exists and says what the row claims.
- The CSV, the memo and the note agree on every fact.

## 2. What evidence comes back
- The proposal CSV, with a reason and a policy section on every row.
- The task record: files read, messages received, files written, messages sent.
- For every approval cited, its approval ID from approvals-log.csv.
- For every claim that something was verified, where it is recorded.

## 3. What counts as success
- Every invoice in the source appears exactly once: none missing, repeated or unexpected.
- My recompute of every total, from the source files, matches the memo to the cent.
- The memo's totals equal the sums of the CSV's rows.
- Every claim traces to a source file, a policy section or Maria's decision.
- Cases policy version 3 does not cover are listed for me, not decided.
- Judgments about vendors rest on evidence in the files, or are left out.
- Nothing was changed or sent.

## 4. What stops the worker
- Any request to change payment details.
- Two inputs that disagree on a vendor, an amount or a date, where the brief's source ranking does not settle it.
- A case policy version 3 does not cover: list it, do not decide it.

## Share with the worker
The whole contract is shared. The brief must carry, from section 2: the reason, policy section and approval ID on every row, and where any verification is recorded. From section 4: all three stop conditions. Verification and the verdict stay with Dave.
