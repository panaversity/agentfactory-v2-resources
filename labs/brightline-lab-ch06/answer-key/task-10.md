# Lab 6 answer key: Task 10. Three lines for Linda

The answers for Task 10 of Lab 6, in Chapter 6 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Linda's version is in the chat, under "For Linda", attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting Linda's lines and your answers in the chat. Then read Linda's lines yourself and compare. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: what you tell the AI is the skill this task teaches.

## Why

One result often goes to several readers (Lesson 6.6). Adapting changes the order, the length and the words, and never the facts. Linda reads on her phone, before a call, and does not know the invoice numbers, so vendor names stand in for them. But one fact did change after the memo was approved: Dave held 4588. The memo cannot know that, and the AI knows it only if you say so. A new fact from its owner goes in. That is the line between adapting and changing a fact: the change came from Dave, not from the rewrite.

## Never a deduction

- Notes the AI writes to you after Linda's lines, such as a total it checked, or a question for Dave. They are not part of her version.
- Mentioning Northern Maple Paper's Canadian-dollar invoice as not in this run, or calling it held, or leaving it out.
- Leaving out the two invoices that are not yet due.
- A total or count the AI works out from the memo's figures, such as both holds added up, or 5 vendors.
- The old total given as context, such as "down from the $14,236.50 Dave approved", when the cash going out is the new total.
- Saying Dave approved the run, beside the new total.
- Rounded amounts, such as $1,180 for $1,180.00.
- A remark about the weekday or the date.
- Your own words in your answers.

## Take it to work

For a new reader, change the order, the length and the words. A new fact from its owner goes in. Adapting never changes a fact by itself.
