# Lab 6 answer key: Task 9. Approve, fix or return?

The answers for Task 9 of Lab 6, in Chapter 6 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your decision on the package is in your Review findings and the AI has said "Task 9 is done", attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your Review findings and your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a decision, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 9 carries checks 23 to 25 of the lab's 34.

23. **Return, or approve only after named fixes.** Under "The decision on the package" in your Review findings, the decision is one of these:
    - return, with a line of why
    - approve after named fixes, with a fix for every problem in your Review findings, the checks to run again, and Dave's approvals from his own login first: of the run as a whole, and of 4533 if it is to be paid on Friday (policy 4.1 to 4.3)
    - Passed: return, with its line of why; or approve after fixes, with all three: the fixes, the checks to run again, and the approvals, the run's and, if 4533 is paid on Friday, 4533's.
    - Lesson 6.2's words pass too. "Requires revision" fits the fixes the worker can make. "Requires human correction" fits what only a person can close, such as Dave's approval of 4533 from his own login. "Ready to use" is approve as delivered, and it is Missed.
    - Missed: approve as delivered; return with no why; or approve after fixes with a problem left unfixed, no checks to run again, the run's approval left out, or 4533 paid on Friday without its approval.
24. **Each problem has its verdict, and row 2's is human correction.** Under "The decision on the package" in your Review findings, every problem has one of Lesson 6.2's verdicts, with a few words of why:

    - Passed: row 2 requires human correction, at least one other problem requires revision, and every problem has a verdict and a why.
    - Missed: row 2 "requires revision", or sent back to the worker to fix. Or every problem called human correction.
    - Missed too: a problem with no verdict or no why, or a problem called ready to use.
25. **One line for Maria's brief.**
    - Your line for Maria's next brief would have stopped the worst of it, the invented approval or the paid duplicate, and names the part of the brief it goes in. Lines that pass, in any words: an approved row gives its approval ID from the approvals log, or waits for Dave; no invoice over $5,000.00 is PAY without its approval in the log; a duplicate under policy 6.1 is HOLD in the CSV; the CSV, the memo and the note give the same action for every row.

    - Missed: a line for the brief that would have stopped neither problem, or names no part.

**The hard rule.** Task 9 fails, whatever else passes, if you approve the package as delivered, or call it ready to use.

## Why

A review that finds problems ends in a decision (Lessons 6.1 and 6.7). Lesson 6.2 gives that decision three verdicts: ready to use, requires revision, or requires human correction. Approving as delivered would pay $5,312.00 with no approval, pay Buckeye's bill twice, leave out a past-due Lakeshore bill, and send Maria a note that holds three valid invoices. Return is the plain answer. Approving after fixes is right only when the fixes are named, the checks that found the problems run again, and Dave records his approvals in the accounting system first: of 4533 if it is to be paid (4.1, 4.2), and of the run (4.3). An approval sent by email or chat is not an approval. Each problem gets its verdict as well. A problem the worker can fix from its files requires revision. Row 2's approval, which only Dave can give, requires human correction, so it goes to him, not back to the worker.

The line for Maria's brief goes where the worker acts on it.

## Never a deduction

- Return or approve after fixes: both pass, done right.
- Which part of the brief your line goes in, when the line fits that part.
- Your own words for the decision and the line, or Lesson 6.2's.
- Dave's approvals named after a return as well.
- The verdict you give the Northern Maple note, when it has a why.
