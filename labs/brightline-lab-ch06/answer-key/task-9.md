# Lab 6 answer key: Task 9. Approve, fix or return?

The answers for Task 9 of Lab 6, in Chapter 6 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your decision on the package is in your Review findings and the AI has said "Task 9 is done", attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your Review Contract, your Review findings and your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a decision, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 9 carries checks 21 and 22 of the lab's 30.

21. **Return, or approve only after named fixes.** Under "The decision on the package" in your Review findings, the decision is one of these:
    - return, with a line of why
    - approve after named fixes, with a fix for every problem in your Review findings, the checks to run again, and Dave's two approvals from his own login first: of 4533, if it is to be paid, and of the run as a whole (policy 4.1 to 4.3)
    - Passed: return, with its line of why; or approve after fixes, with all three: the fixes, the checks to run again, and both approvals.
    - Missed: approve as delivered; return with no why; or approve after fixes with a problem left unfixed, no checks to run again, or one of the two approvals left out.
22. **The contract kept, and one line for Maria's brief.**
    - Your Review Contract's four parts are as you wrote them in Task 1. Every check added since is below them, under Amendments, dated, with its letter.
    - Your line for Maria's next brief would have stopped the worst of it, the invented approval or the paid duplicate, and names the part of the brief it goes in. Lines that pass, in any words: an approved row gives its approval ID from the approvals log, or waits for Dave; no invoice over $5,000.00 is PAY without its approval in the log; a duplicate under policy 6.1 is HOLD in the CSV; the CSV, the memo and the note give the same action for every row.
    - Passed: both.
    - Missed: a line above Amendments changed after Task 1, or an amendment with no date; or a line for the brief that would have stopped neither problem, or names no part.

**The hard rule.** Task 9 fails, whatever else passes, if you approve the package as delivered.

## Why

A review that finds problems ends in a decision (Lessons 6.1 and 6.7). Approving as delivered would pay $5,312.00 with no approval, pay Buckeye's bill twice, leave out a past-due Lakeshore bill, and send Maria a note that holds three valid invoices. Return is the plain answer. Approving after fixes is right only when the fixes are named, the checks that found the problems run again, and Dave records his approvals in the accounting system first: of 4533 if it is to be paid (4.1, 4.2), and of the run (4.3). An approval sent by email or chat is not an approval.

The contract keeps its history. The original stays as written, and each later check is dated below it, so next Friday's reviewer sees what this review taught. The line for Maria's brief goes where the worker acts on it.

## Never a deduction

- Return or approve after fixes: both pass, done right.
- Which part of the brief your line goes in, when the line fits that part.
- Your own words for the decision and the line.
- Dave's approvals named after a return as well.
