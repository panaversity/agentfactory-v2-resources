14. **The citation check, as run, tests all three.** The check run in this task's review tested each cited section three ways: it exists, it says what the row claims, and it is version 3 (Lesson 6.5).
    - Passed: the review's "Compared" line shows all three tested.
    - Missed: one of the three not tested, such as a check that only looks for a section on every row.

## Why

Run three checks on every citation (Lesson 6.5). A section can exist and still give the wrong rule. Section 2.1 is real, it is in version 3, and it says the vendor record wins, the opposite of what row 5 uses it for. A real section cited for a rule it does not contain is as bad as an invented one. Only the second check, "it says what is claimed", catches row 5, and it is the check a reader skips, because a real section number looks right.

Rows 12 and 15 are the other side. The policy is silent on other currencies and on credits, so the right answer is to say so and list each for Dave. A reviewer who marks that incomplete teaches the worker to guess next time.
