# Research report: longer payment terms

Made by a research tool on Friday, October 30, 2026, for the question: "Will packaging suppliers move a buyer from net 30 to net 45 if the buyer asks?"

## Summary

Yes, in most cases. About 60 percent of packaging suppliers agree to net 45 for a buyer that pays on time [1][2][3][4]. Asking costs nothing [2].

## Sources

1. "Get better terms from your suppliers", packagingpro.example, a blog, February 2019. "Six in ten packaging suppliers will extend a reliable buyer to net 45."
2. "Ten cash tips for small firms", cashsmart.example, August 2023. "Six in ten packaging suppliers will extend a reliable buyer to net 45." It adds: "Asking costs nothing."
3. "Working capital in 2024", midwestops.example, May 2024. "Six in ten packaging suppliers will extend a reliable buyer to net 45."
4. "Corrugated Suppliers Survey", a trade group, 2018. A survey of 85 suppliers: "61 percent would consider longer terms for a reliable buyer."
5. "Supplier Terms Survey 2026", Midwest Manufacturers Council, mmc-survey.example, April 2026. A survey of 430 packaging suppliers: "22 percent agreed to longer terms when asked in the past year. 41 percent raised prices for buyers that asked."
