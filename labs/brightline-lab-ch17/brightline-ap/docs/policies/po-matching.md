# Purchase order matching

Owner: Ana Morales, AP analyst.

Every invoice must match a purchase order (PO) before it is paid.
An invoice matches when its vendor and amount equal the PO's vendor and amount.
An invoice over its PO amount, by any amount, is held, and the AP lead asks the buyer why.
An invoice under its PO amount may be paid, and the AP lead notes the difference.
An invoice with no PO is held until the buyer raises one.
Utilities due on receipt still need a PO, raised once a year as a blanket order.
