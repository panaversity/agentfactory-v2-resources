# Lab 1: a real job, checked

Chapter 1 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company here is invented.

**Start with `LAB.md`.** It is the book's page for this lab, [Lab 1: a real job, checked](https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/ai-worker-paradigm/from-chatbots-to-ai-workers/lab/), as a file, so you can do the lab without the book.

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `LAB.md` | The lab: the job, and fourteen tasks in two sittings. Every task but Tasks 7 and 10 has its own answer key. Task 8 runs in a desktop app, and saves a file in a folder you make. Part B, Tasks 11 to 14, uses newer tools: some need a paid plan, and Task 14 needs a Google account. |
| `inputs.zip` | One folder, `inputs`, with `invoices/` (15 PDFs) and `purchase-orders.csv`. To check the AI's work, open it on your computer. |

**Give the AI only `inputs.zip` and, at each check, that task's answer key.** Never upload `LAB.md` or this file: they link every answer key.

The answer keys are not in the zip. Each task's key is a one-click download, linked from `LAB.md` at that task's check step: you download it when the task is done, and give it to your AI to check.
