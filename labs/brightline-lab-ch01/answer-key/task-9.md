# Lab 1 answer key: Task 9. Where each result lives

The answers for Task 9 of Lab 1, in Chapter 1 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Task 9's run is done, attach this file in the same conversation, with the lab's check prompt. Your AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your answers from the conversation as proof. Then read the checks yourself and compare. You have the final say.

## The checks

Task 9 carries checks 19 and 20 of the lab's 28.

19. **Result 5 is "the AI's workspace, goes", and results 1 to 4 each stay, in a place that fits.**

| Result | Place | Stays or goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The Task 1 total, as text | My account: it is part of the conversation | Stays |
| 2. The Task 2 spreadsheet, as a file in the chat | My account: a file card in the chat that made it. "My own computer" also passes, if you downloaded it | Stays |
| 3. The Task 5 doc | My account: the Artifacts section on Claude, or Library on ChatGPT. "My own computer" or "a connected app" also passes, if that is where it went | Stays |
| 4. The Friday file in your Brightline AP folder | My own computer | Stays |
| 5. A file the AI made in Task 2 and never delivered | The AI's workspace | Goes. It lived only there, and the workspace is wiped when the session ends |

- A result 2 or 3 placed in the AI's workspace is a miss. It was delivered to you, so it is kept in your account.
- If the AI said in Task 2 that it made no other files, result 5 is still "the AI's workspace, goes": that is where such a file would live.
- If you did not do Task 8, result 4 is skipped.

20. **Records D and E are official, and A, B and C are not, each with a reason in its spirit.**

| Record | Official? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. What the AI remembers about you | No | It belongs to one login. It helps the AI, but it is not the company's record. |
| B. A shared project your team keeps files in | No | It belongs to a team. It lasts, but nobody made it the company's record. |
| C. The Task 5 doc | No | It lasts, and you can open it again, but it is a copy you made. Nobody approved it as the record. |
| D. Brightline's accounting system | Yes | It is the live state of the ledger: what was paid, and what is owed now. |
| E. Brightline's approved AP policy | Yes | It is the company's official record of its policy. |

- A short reason is enough, such as "one person's", "a team's copy" or "the real ledger".

## Worth noting, not scored

Durable is not the same as official. A result can last for years in your account and still not be what the company treats as true. Chapter 4 adds three layers for that. KSoR governs what an AI Worker may know. DSoR governs what it may do. SSoR tracks where the work stands.
