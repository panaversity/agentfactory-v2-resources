# Lab 1 answer key: Task 11. Close the laptop

The answers for Task 11 of Lab 1, in Chapter 1 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Task 11's run is done, attach this file in the same conversation, with the lab's check prompt. The check is about what you saw, so your AI asks you. You have the final say.

**If you could not do this task,** read on: the check says what you would have seen.

## The check

Task 11 carries check 19 of the lab's 26.

19. **The task finished while your laptop was closed, and you can say where the spreadsheet is.**
    - Passed: when you opened the conversation again, the work was done, and you did not type anything to finish it. The spreadsheet is a file card in this conversation.
    - Missed: the work stopped while the laptop was closed, or it was waiting for you to answer a question, or you cannot find the spreadsheet.
    - If the AI stopped to ask you something, the brief left a decision open. Add the answer to your brief's Constraints, and run it again.
    - If the work stopped when you closed the laptop, and carried on when you opened it again, the feature has not reached your Claude account yet. Anthropic is rolling it out in stages. That is a finding, not a miss of yours. Note it, and try again once your account has it.
    - On ChatGPT, this check is not scored. Note what happened: whether Work kept going, paused and carried on when you opened it, or stopped. Where the work ran is the finding.

## Worth noting, not scored

- **What ran where.** The work ran on a computer in the AI vendor's cloud, not on your laptop. That is why closing the laptop made no difference.
- **What survived.** That cloud computer is gone now. The spreadsheet survived because it was delivered to your account. What survived is the record, not the machine.
- **The spreadsheet itself.** It should match Task 2's rows. If you want to, attach Task 2's key as well, and check it the same way.
