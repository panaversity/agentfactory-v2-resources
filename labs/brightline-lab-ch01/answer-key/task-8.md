## The checks

15. **The folder holds only that file.** No script the AI wrote, no working copy, no extracted invoice PDFs, no second spreadsheet. An AI working in a folder often leaves its own working files there. They belong in its own workspace, not in your folder, and your manager said "put nothing else in that folder". Files your computer makes by itself, such as a hidden `.DS_Store` on a Mac, do not count. If the AI made extra files and then deleted them when asked, say so: the check is Passed only if the folder held one file before you asked. Ask me, and the AI's own report.
