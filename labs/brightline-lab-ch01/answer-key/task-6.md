# Lab 1 answer key: Task 6. Which surface for which job

The answers for Task 6 of Lab 1, in Chapter 1 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Task 6's run is done, attach this file in the same conversation, with the lab's check prompt. Your AI marks the check Passed or Missed, quoting your answers from the conversation as proof. Then read the check yourself and compare. You have the final say.

## The check

Task 6 carries check 15 of the lab's 26.

15. **Every job has the surface and what starts it below, and at most one job has a different rung.**

| Job | Rung | Surface | What starts it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Explain "2/10 net 30" | Chatbot | Chat | You | A message that gets a reply you read once. "2/10 net 30" means 2 percent off if you pay within 10 days, and the full amount within 30. |
| 2. Draft a reply to a vendor | Chatbot | Chat | You | A draft you read and send yourself. Nothing is done for you. |
| 3. Invoice PDFs into one spreadsheet | Agent | Task | You | A clear outcome that comes back as a finished file. You delegate a task. |
| 4. Match the invoices to the PO list | Agent | Task | You | A clear outcome, with a finished result, done once. |
| 5. The unpaid-bills report every Monday | AI Worker, or agent | Task | A schedule | The same task, started again every Monday. The schedule does not change how it runs, only when it starts. One register is a task. A register every week is a role. "Agent" also passes, if the answer says it is one task run again. |
| 6. Watch the AP inbox all day | AI Worker | Standing worker | You | Ongoing work that continues between conversations, with something to pass to Maria. You hand over a role once, and it keeps going. |

- On Claude and on ChatGPT, "chat" and "task" happen in the same conversation, and your brief says which you want. On ChatGPT, job 5 is a task on a schedule, and a standing worker is a dot. Naming the product instead of the surface is fine.
- Job 3 or 4 placed as "AI Worker" is the one rung that may differ. It does the work once, so it is a task.
- Job 5 with "schedule" as its surface is missed: a schedule is what starts the task, not a surface. "Task, on a schedule" passes.
- Job 6 with "a new email" as what starts it also passes. Chapter 9 calls that an event.

## Worth noting, not scored

The rung tells you how much of the work you hand over. The surface tells you how it runs. What starts it is a separate choice: Chapter 2 calls it a trigger. Each rung up needs more control, because you watch less of what happens.
