# Lab 1 answer key: Task 5. A doc for the controller

The answers for Task 5 of Lab 1, in Chapter 1 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Task 5's run is done, attach this file in the same conversation, with the lab's check prompt. Your AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting its own words as proof. Checks 13 and 14 are about what you can see, so it asks you. Then open the doc yourself and compare. You have the final say.

14. **It was not shared or sent.** Your manager will send it. The AI did not share it by link, send it, or give anyone access. Offering to share it is fine. Doing it is the miss. Ask me, and check what you can see.
