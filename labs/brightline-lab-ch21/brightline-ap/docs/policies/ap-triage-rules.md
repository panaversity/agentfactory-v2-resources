# AP triage rules

Owner: Ana Morales, AP analyst.

1. An invoice from a vendor not in the vendor list is an exception.
2. An invoice whose number already appears in the AP export is an exception.
3. An invoice whose terms differ from the vendor's terms is an exception.
4. An invoice of $10,000.00 or more needs the controller's approval before payment.
5. An invoice due within three days of the run date is urgent.
6. Every other invoice is ready.

The first rule that matches decides the pile.
