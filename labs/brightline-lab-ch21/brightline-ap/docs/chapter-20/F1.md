# F1. Accounts, keys and spending limits (by you)

**Claude.** Sign in to the Claude Console at `platform.claude.com`, add API credits on the **Billing** page, make a workspace for this lab, and set its monthly spending limit (**Workspaces**, select it, **Spend limits**: the Default Workspace cannot have one). On the **API keys** page, choose **Create key**, scoped to that workspace, with an expiry of 30 days. Copy it once into `.secrets/anthropic.env` with your editor.

**OpenAI.** Sign in to the OpenAI platform dashboard, add credits under billing, make a project for this lab, and set its spend limit to $5 with a hard limit (**Project settings**, **Limits**, **Spend**, **Edit spend limit**, then turn on **Enforce a hard limit**). Make a key for that project, and copy it once into `.secrets/openai.env`.

**Why each key gets its own small limit.** A key that leaks spends money until someone notices. A small limit on its own project or workspace caps the damage, and the lab's own cost cap (20.8) caps each run.
