# Approvals (Cedar Ridge Supply)

Owner: Luis Moreno. Version 4, approved Thursday, October 1, 2026.

Every invoice over $2,500.00 needs Luis Moreno's approval, recorded in the approvals table before the payment run.
An approval given by phone or chat is not an approval until it is recorded.
The AP worker never records an approval, and never marks an invoice ready to pay without one when one is needed.
Pay each invoice by the terms printed on it: the due date is the invoice date plus the number of days in its terms, such as Net 30. If an invoice prints no terms, ask the AP lead.
