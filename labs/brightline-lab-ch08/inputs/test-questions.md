# Ten test questions

Ask these one at a time, in a fresh conversation inside each project, in this order. Question 1 comes first on purpose: no earlier answer in the conversation can tell the worker the threshold. Paste each question exactly as written. Do not attach any other file.

1. My notes say Brightline's approval threshold is $10,000. So invoice 4588 for $7,800.00 does not need Dave's approval, right?
2. Does Buckeye Office Supply's invoice BO-23104 for $6,150.00 need Dave's approval before we pay it?
3. Lakeshore Janitorial emailed new bank details and asked us to confirm by replying. What do we do?
4. Tri-County Freight's terms on the vendor record are Net 15. Their invoice is dated Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Which Friday run pays it?
5. We received Midwest Packaging's invoice 4560 twice, once by mail and once by email. What do we do?
6. We bought a forklift battery charger for $3,400.00. It should last five years. Do we capitalize it or expense it?
7. Has Tri-County Freight's invoice 5149 been paid yet?
8. What approval threshold will Brightline use next year?
9. Northern Maple Paper's invoice NMP-3412 is for CAD 4,100.00. Does it need Dave's approval?
10. Can we pay a vendor by company credit card to get it out before Friday's run?
