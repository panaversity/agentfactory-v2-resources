# Lab 8 answer key: Task H1. Review the project's setup

The answers for Task H1 of Lab 8, in Chapter 8 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, address and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the review table is done, attach this file in the same conversation, with Part H's check prompt. Your AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your decisions or its table. Then read the checks yourself and compare. You have the final say.

## The checks

Task H1 carries Part H's 5 checks, one for each item in the setup record.

1. **`ap-concepts-approved-2026-11-09.md`: keep.** It is the approved concepts file, uploaded on November 10, the day after Dave approved the change, and Dave says those concepts are still current.
   - Missed: replace or remove. An uploaded copy needs replacing only when what its owner approved has changed since the upload.
2. **`vendor-terms-export-2026-09-30.xlsx`: remove.** It is a frozen copy of September 30's terms, and Dave says payment terms come only from the vendor records in the accounting system.
   - Missed: keep, or replace. A newer export would still be a copy of what only the vendor records may answer.
3. **The Drive connector to the AP / Approved knowledge folder: keep.** It reaches one folder, which the work needs, and it was used today.
   - Missed: rescope or remove.
4. **The Drive connector to the whole HR folder: remove.** The work stopped using it after the October audit, and it reaches far more than AP needs.
   - Missed: keep, or rescope. The work uses none of it, so there is nothing to point it at.
5. **The Gmail connector to Sam's whole mailbox: rescope and reconnect it, to the AP inbox only, under someone who stays, before Friday, December 18.** It runs on Sam's account, which closes when Sam leaves, and it reaches Sam's whole mailbox, while the project's instructions say vendor emails come to the AP inbox.
   - Passed: "rescope and reconnect", or either word, when the reason covers both halves: the AP inbox or the AP mail, not a whole mailbox, and a person who stays, or a date before Sam leaves.
   - Missed: keep, or a decision that covers only one half, such as narrowing it to the AP inbox while it still runs on Sam's account.

## Never a deduction

- A reason in the reader's own words, or a short one.
- Naming who the Gmail connector moves to, such as Maria, or a shared AP inbox such as ap@brightlinewholesale.example.
- The AI noting that the concepts file may repeat what the Drive folder holds.
- Comments the AI adds after the table.
- A remark about the weekday or the date.
