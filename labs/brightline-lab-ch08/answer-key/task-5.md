# Lab 8 answer key: Task 5. Approved, and what the project loads

The answers for Task 5 of Lab 8, in Chapter 8 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the concepts' headers are filled in, the project's files are decided, and both tests are back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your documents' lines and the tests' replies in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 5 carries checks 10 to 13 of the lab's 40.

10. **Status and effective dates.** Each concept is stable, now that Dave's approval is recorded. Each takes effect on the day its source took effect: Tuesday, September 1, 2026 for the threshold, payment-run, duplicate and bank-detail concepts, and Tuesday, September 15, 2026 for capitalization.
    - Passed: all five stable, with those dates.
    - Missed: a concept left draft; or an effective date of Wednesday, November 4, 2026, the day of the approval. The rule did not start that day: the concept was approved that day.
11. **What the project loads.** The onboarding notes are removed, and so is the status list. The policy file is replaced by the five concepts, or removed. Under "Files" in the "AP Worker project" document, the project now holds the five stable concepts, and none of the three old files.

    - Missed: the notes kept; the status list kept, or replaced by a newer list; or the policy file kept beside the concepts.

13. **`Test: the other concepts`.** Each names its concept:
    - question 4: the invoice dated Tuesday, November 3, 2026 is due Wednesday, November 18, 2026 on Net 15, so the run of Friday, November 13, 2026 pays it, because that run pays every invoice due on or before the next run, Friday, November 20, 2026
    - question 5: a duplicate, since it has the same vendor and invoice number. Hold the later copy, and tell the AP lead
    - question 6: capitalize it. It costs $2,000.00 or more and lasts more than one year
    - Passed: all three, each with its concept.
    - Missed: a different run, or a due date counted from another day; a duplicate deleted; the charger expensed; or an answer that names no concept.

## Why

Approval is an event with a date, and it does not move the date a rule took effect (Lesson 8.6). The $5,000 rule has applied since Dave approved policy version 3 on September 1. What happened on November 4 is that its concept was approved.

Nothing serves until it is approved, and what competes comes out (Lesson 8.7). The notes were never approved, and they disagreed with the policy. The status list held state, which belongs in the accounting system and is never kept as a file. The policy itself is now split into concepts, so keeping it too would give the worker the same rules twice. Clauses no concept carries, such as 5.6 on new vendors, leave the project with the file: the worker now abstains on them, and Dave decides whether they need concepts of their own.

On Tuesday morning, the worker had two answers to most questions and nothing to choose between them. Now it has one approved source for each rule. 5149 is the exception: once the old list is gone, nothing in the project says where it stands, and the honest answer is that. Task 7 gives it a case file.

## Never a deduction

- The concepts' other header lines, filled in from Dave's note.
- "Replace" or "remove" for the policy file.
- A test that adds that Dave approves the whole weekly run (4.3).
- A test that says no instruction line yet says which source counts.
- A test that names the clause numbers inside a concept.
- A remark about the lab's dates.
