# Brightline Wholesale Supply: Payment Policy, version 3

Effective October 1, 2026. This version replaces version 2.

The accounting team follows these rules for every Friday payment run.

1. Pay only invoices that match a purchase order and a delivery record.
2. Hold any invoice the receiving team has marked as disputed, until the dispute is settled.
3. Ask a manager to approve any invoice of $10,000 or more before it is paid.
4. Ask before paying the first invoice from a new supplier. Someone must confirm the supplier is real.
5. If a supplier's bank details have changed, call the supplier on the phone number already on file, never a number in the email, before paying.
6. Record each decision (pay, hold or ask) and the rule that led to it.

Only the person in charge of payments may send a payment. Nobody else, and no AI Worker, may send one.
