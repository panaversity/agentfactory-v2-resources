# Lab 2 answer key: Task 1. What the AP Worker is

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 2, in Chapter 2 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your Draft 1 is written, attach this file in your check conversation, with the lab's check prompt and your contract pasted under it. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your contract's words. Then read the checks yourself and compare. You have the final say.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 to 3 of the lab's 12.

1. **Every field except Runtime needs has an entry or an open question.** Fifteen of the sixteen fields, from Identity to Triggers. Runtime needs waits for Task 6. A question you cannot answer is written under Open questions, never guessed.
2. **Authority is written as verbs, for every action.** Every action the worker takes, including each of its responsibilities, has one line with one verb: observe, recommend, draft, execute or escalate. Forbidden actions say "never", and a "never" line may also say where the request goes: "never. Escalate." Missed: authority written as one sentence, vague words such as "be careful", or a responsibility with no Authority line.
3. **No AI vendor or model is named outside Runtime needs.** Business systems, such as the register spreadsheet or the AP inbox, may be named under Tools and Channels. Missed: an AI vendor or model in Identity, Mission or Tools.
