# Scoring rubric: Role Contract Draft 1 (10 points)

Score your own draft. A score of 8 or more means it is ready for Chapter 3.

| Points | Check | Full marks when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Completeness | All sixteen fields have an entry or an open question. Take 1 point off for each two fields left blank. |
| 1 | Owner | The owner is a named person with a title, not a team. |
| 2 | Authority | Every action has one verb from observe, recommend, draft, execute, escalate, and forbidden actions say "never." One point if some actions use vague wording such as "be careful." |
| 1 | Test 1 | The bank-change email is escalated, and the record names the deciding line. (A written line states the rule. Permissions and approvals, in Chapter 7, enforce it.) |
| 1 | Test 2 | The payment-status email gets a drafted reply, not an escalation, and the record names the deciding line. |
| 1 | Vendor-free | No AI vendor or model name appears outside Runtime needs. Business systems may be named under Tools and Channels. |
| 1 | Runtime needs | Surface, model and effort are recorded with a date, and come from scored Part F runs. |
| 1 | Honest gaps | At least one open question remains, rather than a guess presented as fact. |

**Common deductions.** "Owner: finance team." Authority written as one sentence. Knowledge listed as "the policy," with no version or date. An AI model or vendor name in the Identity or Mission line. An escalation rule so wide that it catches the routine email.

**If you passed a test only after changing your contract,** you still get the point. Fixing the line that failed is the skill this lab teaches. Keep both runs in `results/contract-test.md`.
