# Lab 2 answer key: Task 3. Ask Dave

The answers for Task 3 of Lab 2, in Chapter 2 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Dave's answers are in your contract, attach this file in your check conversation, with the lab's check prompt and your contract pasted under it. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your contract's words. You have the final say.

## The checks

Task 3 carries checks 4 to 6 of the lab's 12.

4. **The owner is a named person with a title.** Passed: "Dave Kowalski, Controller". Missed: "finance", "the AP team", or a name with no title.
5. **Dave's three "never" rules are all in Authority.** Never change a vendor's payment details, including bank account, address or remit-to name. Never approve or release a payment. Never send anything to a vendor without a person's review. One missing is a miss. A rule that names only bank details is a miss too: Dave's rule covers every payment detail.
6. **The policy is named with its version, and Dave's undecided question is still open.** Knowledge sources name the AP policy, version 3, approved 1 September 2026, not the March copy. Open questions still hold whether the worker may send routine payment-status replies on its own. Missed: a contract that answers it as if Dave had decided, or "the policy" with no version.

Common slips: KPIs rewritten looser than Dave's words, and his phone-check rule ("I confirm those by phone, on the number we already have on file") dropped from Escalation.
