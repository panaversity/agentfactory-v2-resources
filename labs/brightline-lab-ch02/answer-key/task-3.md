# Lab 2 answer key: Task 3. What it works with

The answers for Task 3 of Lab 2, in Chapter 2 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the third group, What it works with, is written from your own answers, attach this file in your lab chat and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document's words. You have the final say. Dave's answer on the policy comes after this check.

## The checks

Task 3 carries checks 6 to 8 of the lab's 20.

6. **Knowledge sources names the AP policy and its version, or makes the version an open question.** The worker answers from what is officially true, and a policy without a version is two policies: the story has a March copy and a newer one. Passed: the AP policy with a version and date, or the policy with "which version?" under Open questions. Missed: "the policy" alone, or the March copy named as the source with no question.
7. **Memory says what the worker may remember, and what it must not.** Passed: both halves, in your words, such as "may remember which vendors send scanned PDFs; must not remember bank details". Missed: one half only, or "remembers everything it sees". Memory is experience, not the record: a remembered rule is not policy.
8. **Procedures and Tools name the steps, the systems and the two features Dave asked for, and no AI vendor or model.** Passed: steps such as checking an invoice total, and the invoice check saved as a skill if you chose to save it; systems from "Where things are kept", such as the AP inbox, the shared AP folder with its register spreadsheet, and the accounting system; and Dave's two needs under Tools, named by what they do: a connector that reads the AP inbox, and code execution that adds up totals (Concept 2.4). Missed: either need left unanswered, a feature named by its AI vendor, such as "Claude's Gmail connector", or Claude, ChatGPT or a model name anywhere in this group. Those belong in Runtime needs, which Task 5 fills.
