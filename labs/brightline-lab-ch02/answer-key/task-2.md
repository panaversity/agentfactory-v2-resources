# Lab 2 answer key: Task 2. What it owes

The answers for Task 2 of Lab 2, in Chapter 2 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the second group, What it owes, is written from your own answers, attach this file in your lab chat and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document's words. You have the final say. Dave's KPIs come after this check, so it grades what you wrote, not his.

## The checks

Task 2 carries checks 4 and 5 of the lab's 20.

4. **Responsibilities are one line per recurring duty, each saying where its result is kept, from Brightline's places.** The inventory gives six duties; which ones the worker takes is your call. Passed: each duty you kept has its own line, and each line names a place from "Where things are kept", such as the register in the shared AP folder, or a draft reply in the AP inbox. A duty you left with a person or with Dave is not listed, or is listed as theirs: both pass. The inventory's last row, preparing Friday's payment run, is the controller's job today, so leaving it to Dave passes, and so does keeping it with its list in the shared AP folder. Missed: the duties as one sentence, or a duty the worker keeps with no place for its result.
5. **KPIs are results the business can count, or a question for Dave.** "What went wrong in September" lists four problems. Passed: KPIs that count something, such as a number, a deadline or zero of something, each preventing one of those problems, such as duplicate payments made, late fees paid, or the hour the register is ready. Also passed: "Dave decides", with the question under Open questions, because KPIs are how the business measures the role, and that is the owner's call. Missed: "good work", "accurate", "quickly" or "fast" with no measure beside it. A KPI nobody can count is not a KPI.

Dave's answer comes after this check. Put his four KPIs beside yours: each one prevents one of September's problems. A problem you had no KPI for is the lesson.
