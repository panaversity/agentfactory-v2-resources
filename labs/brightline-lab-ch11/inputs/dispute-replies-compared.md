# Two replies to the same kind of dispute

Both answer a vendor who asks why a payment was short.

## Maria's reply (written by hand, December)

> Hi Tom, thanks for checking. Invoice 3977 was $2,150.00. Your terms with us are 1 percent 15, net 45. We paid on day 9, so we took the 1 percent discount of $21.50 and paid $2,128.50. That matches your terms, so nothing more is owed. Maria, Accounts Payable

## Jordan's reply (drafted by the worker from Jordan's brief, January)

> Dear Vendor, Brightline's standard terms are 2 percent 10, net 30. As payment was made within 10 days, the early-payment discount was applied. Please contact us with any questions. Regards, Accounts Payable

Same job, two people, two very different results.
