# What happened in the week of January 11, 2027

**Monday, January 11.** Maria spent the afternoon on a large year-end task from her seat on Brightline's company plan: matching twelve months of vendor statements against the register. See `usage-report.md`.

**Tuesday, January 12.** Nine invoice emails arrived. The duplicate check that runs on each invoice email checked none of them. It reports only when it finds a duplicate. One of the nine was Ohio Valley Packaging's invoice 7781, sent again as 7781-R for $4,812.50. Invoice 7781 was already paid on January 7. 7781-R was entered, due January 15, and scheduled for the next payment run on Monday, January 18.

**Wednesday, January 13.** Jordan Ellis asked the worker to "reply to these three vendors about their short payments." All three drafts said Brightline's terms were "2 percent 10, net 30" and that the deduction was the early-payment discount. Maria caught it in review. Nothing was sent.

**Friday, January 8.** After the January 7 run, Maria closed the 2026 register and started "AP Register 2027."

**Thursday, January 14, 7 a.m.** The pre-run note said "No invoices due this week." Maria saw a short note in the usual layout and passed it to Dave. 31 invoices, worth $86,240.15, were due.

**Friday, January 15.** Five vendor replies went out saying "Attached is the remittance advice." Nothing was attached. Maria approved the batch without opening an attachment. On January 4, IT reset all staff passwords.

**Friday afternoon.** Dave Kowalski, the controller, said: "Switch to the most expensive model, and Maria, rewrite every brief from scratch."
