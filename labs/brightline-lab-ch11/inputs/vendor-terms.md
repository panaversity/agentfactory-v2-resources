# KSoR concept: vendor payment terms

**Status:** stable. **Owner:** Maria (AP lead). **Approved by:** Dave Kowalski, December 18, 2026.

Brightline does not have one standard set of terms. Each vendor has its own.

| Vendor | Terms | Discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ohio Valley Packaging | 2 percent 10, net 30 | 2 percent if paid within 10 days |
| Lakeshore Corrugated | net 45 | none |
| Mad River Plastics | 2 percent 10, net 30 | 2 percent if paid within 10 days |
| Great Lakes Fasteners | 1 percent 15, net 45 | 1 percent if paid within 15 days |
| Buckeye Pallet Co. | net 30 | none |
| Midwest Label Works | 2 percent 10, net 30 | 2 percent if paid within 10 days |
| Cardinal Freight | net 15 | none |
| Franklin Electric Supply | 2 percent 10, net 30 | 2 percent if paid within 10 days |
| All other vendors | net 30 | none, unless a signed agreement says otherwise |

Small suppliers (under $500,000 a year with Brightline) are promised payment within 15 days of receiving their invoice.
