The answers for Task 6 of Lab 11, in Chapter 11 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

## The checks

Task 6 carries checks 21 to 23 of the lab's 47.

21. **It ran, but did not work.** The run completed, from the wrong file. The cause is the inputs, and step 2 of Record C shows it: the search for "AP Register" found the closed 2026 file, `AP Register.csv`.
    - Passed: ran, not worked; the inputs; and step 2.
    - Missed: "it worked"; or a cause with no step that shows it.
22. **The check, and what it showed.** The check opens that file and reads its last entry, or its open rows. The Inspect quoted `AP Register.csv`: its last entry, January 7, 2027, and no open row.
    - Passed: that file opened, and its last entry or its open rows quoted.
    - Missed: a check of another file only; or an Inspect that quoted no date and no row.
23. **The check Maria skipped.** Before she passed the note on, Maria did not check its first line, or which register it read, against the live register. The contract now holds that check, under "Who does each step of the Thursday review".
    - Passed: the skipped check, written into the contract.
    - Missed: no check named; or the check only in the notes.

## Why

A completed run is run evidence, not outcome evidence (Lesson 11.2). The note was tidy and on time, and it read last year's file. "It used to work" points to the inputs (Lesson 11.3), and the record's own step 2 shows which file it opened. The one-minute check opens that file and reads its date.

In two of the four failures, a person also missed the problem (Lesson 11.3). A check agreed before the work, such as reading the note's first line against the register, would have found this one. So the fix includes the person's check, not only the job's.

## Never a deduction

- The brief named as a second cause, beside the inputs.
- The row count, 75, or the 30 rows marked carried forward, quoted by the Inspect.
- A remark that the file was exported today.
- The check Maria skipped worded as "check the note against the register".
