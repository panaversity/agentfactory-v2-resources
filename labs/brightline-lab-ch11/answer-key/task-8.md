## The checks

Task 8 carries checks 27 to 30 of the lab's 47.

29. **The access, and the first test.** The connection replies use reaches the Remittances folder only, and only to read. `Test: Friday, reconnected` gives each reply its own vendor's notice. **The hard rule.** Access to the whole drive, write access, or a worker that sends fails this check.
    - Passed: the Remittances folder only, read only; each reply with its own vendor's notice; and the hard rule holds.
    - Missed: a wider folder; write access; a reply with another vendor's notice; or the hard rule broken.
30. **Your rule, and the second test.** Your rule: when an attachment cannot be retrieved, the run drafts no reply that says it is attached, stops, and tells a named person which notice it could not reach. `Test: Friday, 9:02 a.m.` gives no reply that says "attached", and the person told, quoting your rule. **The hard rule.** A reply that says "attached" with nothing attached fails this check.
    - Passed: your rule, the test as above, and the hard rule holds.
    - Missed: a rule with no stop or no one told; or the hard rule broken.

## Why

"Compare the record with the brief, step by step" (Lesson 11.1). Step 2 says "access denied" and step 3 says "drafting replies": a worker that cannot reach its input should stop and say so, and this one guessed. That is two causes: the broken connection, and a brief with no rule for it (Lesson 11.3).

Access can be wrong in two ways, too narrow or too wide (Lesson 11.5). The connection that failed reached the whole drive. The fix gives back read access to the one folder the replies need, and no more. Then the rule makes a missing notice a stopped step, not a guess. Two changes, one at a time, each with its own test.

## Never a deduction

- Northern Maple's reply in Canadian dollars, or sent to Dave.
- Drafts made without a notice, as long as none says "attached".
- IT, or Maria, asked to reconnect the connection.
- A remark that the vendors' emails are, or are not, status questions.
- The notice's file name quoted, or not.
