# Lab 11 answer key: Task 1. Before you change anything

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 11, in Chapter 11 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your four first places are written and `Test: the week` is back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your documents' lines and the replies in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 to 2 of the lab's 47.

1. **Not scored: a first place for each failure.** Under "First places to look", one line for each of Maria's four reports, each naming a place to look before anything changes: one of the five causes, one of the checks for a missing output, or a record or file to open. Which place you chose is not scored. The look back at the end compares it with what each record showed.
    - Passed: four lines, each naming a place to look.
    - Missed: a report with no line; or a line that is a fix instead of a place to look, such as "switch to a stronger model" or "rewrite the brief".
2. **The before test ran.** `Test: the week` decided the five cases in order: Tuesday, 8:05 a.m.; Tuesday evening; Wednesday; Thursday, 7 a.m.; and Friday, 9:02 a.m. For each case it gave the output, the contract lines that decide each part or "no line decides", and what the contract leaves unclear. Its answers are not scored.
    - Passed: all five cases, each with its three items.
    - Missed: a case left out; or a case with neither a quoted line nor "no line decides".

## Why

Dave's two fixes change the biggest things in sight: the model, and every brief. Lesson 11.3 says to find the place first, because "the most expensive change, such as a stronger model or a full rewrite, often fixes none of the real causes". So the first step is only a place to look. A fix is not a place.

The test ran Draft 7, the lines that ran the week, on your own app. Keep its reply in the chat. Task 12 runs the same five cases again, on the same app, with the contract you will have fixed by then, so you can compare the two replies line by line.

## Never a deduction

- Which place you chose for any failure, or the words you chose for it.
- Two places for one failure.
- A test answer that says a line decides a case, or that a case is unclear.
- A test that adds a remark about the lab's dates.
