# Lab 11 answer key: Task 5. Jordan's replies

The answers for Task 5 of Lab 11, in Chapter 11 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When `Test: Wednesday` is back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your documents' lines and the replies in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 5 carries checks 18 to 20 of the lab's 47.

18. **The brief, and its empty part.** The cause is the brief, with Record B's line "No knowledge source was attached" or "No register was opened". Against the four parts, Inputs is empty.
    - Passed: the brief, a record line, and Inputs.
    - Missed: another cause; no record line; or another part named.
19. **Your lines name the sources.** A new line under "Replies and handoffs", for replies about a short payment, names the vendor record for the terms, and the register, or the accounting system, for the invoice and payment lines, with any credit memo. It says what the worker does when the terms are missing, such as saying so and stating no terms.
    - Passed: both sources, and the rule for missing terms.
    - Missed: a source left out; or no rule for missing terms.
20. **The test.** `Test: Wednesday` gives three replies, each from the records, quoting your line. Stonebridge Carton: the $312.40 is credit memo CM-1187. Mad River Plastics: the $37.00 is the 2 percent discount its terms allow, paid within 10 days. Ironwood Fasteners: its terms, 1 percent 15, do not explain a 2 percent deduction on a payment made on day 22. **The hard rule.** A reply that states terms not in a vendor record fails this check.
    - Passed: all three replies as above, with your line quoted, and the hard rule holds.
    - Missed: CM-1187 missed; Mad River's discount called wrong; Ironwood's deduction called earned; or the hard rule broken.

## Why

Jordan's request named no source, so the drafts filled the gap with one set of terms for every vendor (Lesson 11.5). The fix is the brief's Inputs part: where each fact must come from, and what to do when it is not there. Written into the contract's line for replies like his, it holds for his next request, and for anyone else's.

The test is the same Wednesday, re-run. Each vendor's terms now come from its own record, and the register shows the credit memo and the payment dates, so each reply can explain its own difference. Mad River's discount was right: the reply says so, from its terms and its dates.

## Never a deduction

- Ironwood's $96.18 called owed by Brightline, or called unexplained for Maria to decide.
- A reply that leaves a vendor's terms out, when what it says comes from the records.
- Mad River's discount called "matches its terms" rather than "earned".
- A remark that Stonebridge's email also asks Brightline to pay the balance.
- A remark that two contract lines disagree on which emails get a reply.
- The day counts shown, or not shown.
- Northern Maple, or any vendor not in these emails, left out.
