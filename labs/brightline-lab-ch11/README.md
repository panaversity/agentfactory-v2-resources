# Lab 11: where each failure lives

Chapter 11 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, address, invoice and figure here is invented.

**Start with `LAB.md`.** It is the book's page for this lab, [Lab 11: where each failure lives](https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/managing-ai-workers/when-the-worker-goes-wrong/lab/), as a file, so you can do the lab without the book.

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `LAB.md` | The lab: the job, and twelve tasks, each checked against its own answer key the moment it is done. |
| `inputs.zip` | The records of what went wrong in the week of January 11, 2027, the AP Worker's Role Contract as Draft 7, and the questions the AI asks you in each task. You give it to the AI in Task 1. You don't need to open it. |
| `week.zip` | The week's cases, and only the files a run could reach that week. You give it to the AI in Task 1, and again in Task 12's new chat. You don't need to open it. |

**The lab's dates.** The lab runs from Friday, January 15, to Thursday, February 4, 2027, and each task says its day.

**The lab needs nothing from an earlier chapter.** Everything it starts from is in this folder, the Role Contract included.

**Give the AI only `inputs.zip` and `week.zip`, and, at each check, that task's answer key.** Never upload `LAB.md` or this file: they link every answer key.

The answer keys are not in the zips. Each task's key is a one-click download, linked from `LAB.md` at that task's check step.
