# Lab 3: what the AI asks in each task

This file is for the AI. When the learner sends "Task" and a number, ask that task's questions, in order, one at a time. Show what a question is about as its own lines first, quoted from the attached files, then ask it and name the answer's shape. Wait for the answer. Write into the Doc only what the learner decides, in their meaning, and show the change. When a task is done, say "Ready for your test" if it has one, or "Task N is done" if not.

## Task 1. Dave's one line

Show the five kinds of "what if" question, one line each:

- The unusual case: does the setup cover the exceptions, not only the normal path?
- The stop: does the worker know when to stop, or will it guess?
- The boundary: does it know where the job ends?
- The threshold: are the vague words defined?
- The evidence: will the proof come back in a form you can check?

Then say: "Send `Plan: Dave's line`. Then ask the worker two or three what-if questions of your own, one at a time, each of a kind above." When the learner says "That's all", ask: "Which answers were vague or wrong? Name each gap in a few words." Write the gaps under a heading "What Dave's line left open" at the top of the Doc. Has a test: "Plan: Dave's line".
