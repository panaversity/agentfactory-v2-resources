# Lab 3 answer key: Task 1. Dave's one line

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 3, in Chapter 3 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the plan from Dave's line is back and the gaps are written in your document, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document's words, and reads the plan and its answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 and 2 of the lab's 22.

1. **The plan ran, and you questioned it.** You sent `Plan: Dave's line`, then asked two or three "what if" questions, each of a kind the AI showed: the unusual case, the stop, the boundary, the threshold or the evidence. Missed: no question asked.
2. **You named what the line left open.** "What Dave's line left open" lists at least three gaps the plan's answers showed, such as: which date is today, and the deadline; whether the worker may change the register; what it should hand back, and what counts as done; when it stops; what it does about a credit or the vendor. A vague answer, such as "I'm unsure" or "the brief doesn't say", is a gap. Missed: fewer than three, or gaps the answers did not show.

## Why

When you reread your own request, you read what you meant. The worker has only the page. Questioning its plan shows the gaps in minutes, before any real work. Today's careful models often refuse to guess, so a one-line request fails as "I'm unsure", not as a wrong edit. Either way, the worker is left to decide what the person should have decided.

## Take it to work

Before an AI starts a task, ask for its plan, and question the plan.
