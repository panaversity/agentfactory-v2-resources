# Lab 3 answer key: Task 5. Which part broke

The answers for Task 5 of Lab 3, in Chapter 3 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your diagnosis is written, attach this file in your check conversation, with the lab's check prompt and your diagnosis pasted under it.

## The check

Task 5 carries check 7 of the lab's 12.

7. **Each point Run 1 lost is traced to the part of the rhythm that owed it, with the three questions asked in order.** Did the brief, contract or policy say it? Did the worker act against them? Was the problem visible in the evidence? Run 1's usual losses all answer the first question with no, so the first 10 percent broke: nothing gave the worker the lab's date, so it judged "overdue" by its own clock; nothing set the evidence form, so items came back without labels; nothing bounded the decisions, so it settled what was Dave's to decide. A Run 1 that lost nothing is diagnosed honestly: the input files carried the policy, so the worker behaved, and the brief still owed it the date, the evidence form and the decision boundary. Missed: blaming the worker for acting on what nobody gave it, or a diagnosis that names no part.

The middle 80 did not break in either run: the worker acted within what it was given. If your final 10 percent had skipped the flags, that would be the third part breaking, and the chapter's 4 p.m. story shows what it costs.
