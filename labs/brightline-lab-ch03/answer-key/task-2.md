# Lab 3 answer key: Task 2. Intent and scope

The answers for Task 2 of Lab 3, in Chapter 3 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When Intent and Scope are written in your document, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your document's words. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 2 carries checks 3 and 4 of the lab's 22.

3. **The intent says the outcome, why, and by when.** For example: explain the whole $4,000.00 difference between Midwest's statement and the register, so Dave can sign the close on Monday, October 5, 2026 (policy 7.1). Missed: "reconcile the statement" with no outcome, or no deadline.
4. **The scope names today, the files and what is out.** Today is Thursday, October 1, 2026. In: Midwest's statement dated September 28, 2026, as the vendor's claim; the register as of September 30, 2026, as Brightline's record; policy section 7 as the rule; and invoice 4488 as a source document. Each is the source of truth for its own part. Out: other vendors, payments, and changing anything. Missed: no date for today, or a file with no say on what it is the source of truth for.

## Why

A worker running alone cannot ask what you meant. The date is the trap that catches the most briefs: a worker with no date reads its own clock. The scope also stops a willing worker from doing too much, such as tidying three other vendors.
