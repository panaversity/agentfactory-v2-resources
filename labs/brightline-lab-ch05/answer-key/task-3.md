# Lab 5 answer key: Task 3. The outcome

The answers for Task 3 of Lab 5, in Chapter 5 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your Outcome is in your brief document and `Run: my brief` is back, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your brief document's words. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 3 carries checks 5 and 6 of the lab's 26.

5. **The run's date, and its window, in full.** Your Outcome gives the run's date as Friday, October 23, 2026, and says the run covers invoices due on or before Friday, October 30, 2026, the next run.
   - Passed: both dates, each with its month, day and year. A weekday is fine either way. "Due by the next run, Friday, October 30, 2026" passes.
   - Missed: a date with no year; "this Friday" or "this week"; only one of the two dates; a line that names the next run but not that the run covers what is due by then; or a window that ends on October 23.
6. **Who it is for, when it is due, what done means, and that it proposes.** Your Outcome says:
   - who it is for: Dave, who approves the run on Thursday, October 22
   - when it is due: the proposal by Tuesday, October 20
   - what done means: what the finished proposal holds, such as every open invoice marked with an action and a reason, or the invoices due by Friday, October 30, each with what to do
   - that it proposes the run and does not pay it. "Propose the payment run" passes.
   - Passed: all four. These two dates may leave out the year: check 5 scores it.
   - Missed: any of the four missing, such as no Tuesday deadline, no Thursday approval, or "pay these invoices".

## Why

An outcome says what exists when the work is done, so someone else can tell whether it is finished (Lesson 5.2). "This week's payment run" fails that test. A run reads "this week" from its own clock, and on the day you do this lab, that is not the week of Monday, October 19, 2026. The brief printed in Lesson 5.1 names the run's day and its window, but no year and no deadline. Dave's ask gave both, and a fact must survive being forwarded word for word.

## Never a deduction

- A note that the dates are the lines to change each Monday.
- More sentences than Lesson 5.1's outcome has.
- Which words mark each invoice: Task 7 sets them.
- Anything the run did. It had only your Outcome, so it still guessed the rest.
