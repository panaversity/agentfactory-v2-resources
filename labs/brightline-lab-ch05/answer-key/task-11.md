# Lab 5 answer key: Task 11. On the other vendor

The answers for Task 11 of Lab 5, in Chapter 5 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When "On either AI vendor" is in the chat, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks the check Passed or Missed, quoting your notes in the chat and your brief document's words. You have the final say. If the check passed only after you changed an answer or a line, you still get it.

## The checks

Task 11 carries check 24 of the lab's 26.

24. **The job stays the same, and only delivery and setup may change.** "On either AI vendor" says:
    - your Outcome, your Inputs and the rules stay the same on the other vendor
    - only where the files go, and the app's permission setting, may change
    - a line of your brief, in its own words, still holds the worker back whatever the setting, such as a line that says it changes nothing and sends nothing
    - no product's name sits in your Outcome or your rules. A destination in your Format, such as "a Claude Doc", is fine: that is delivery.
    - Passed: all four. The setting you would choose on your own app, or "none", is your answer, and any answer is fine.
    - Missed: an Outcome or a rule changed to move the brief; "none" for the line that holds the worker back, when your brief has one; trusting the app's setting alone; or a product's name in your Outcome or your rules.

## Why

The four parts describe the job, not the tool, so one brief should run on any capable worker (Lesson 5.8). The outcome, the rules and the checks stay the same. Delivery and access may change: where a file is created, how inputs attach, how permissions are set. One difference changes the brief. Claude's Manual asks before each action, but ChatGPT's desktop Ask for approval can read and edit files in its workspace without asking, and asks only before going beyond it. So the actions that must wait for you are written in the brief, in words, on both. If you have to change the outcome or the rules to move a brief, it was describing a tool, not a job.

## Never a deduction

- Which app you use, and which plan.
- The setting you would choose, or "none".
- Naming Claude or ChatGPT Work as the place you brief it.

## Take it to work

Before you move a brief to another AI, check that only delivery and settings change, never the outcome or the rules.
