# Lab 4 answer key: Task 1. Rebuild or carry across?

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When you have answered Task 1's questions, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your map or your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 to 6 of the lab's 13 scored checks, one for each part.

1. **Project instructions: carry, and move to the Role Contract.** They are the worker's job, limits and rules, and their only copy is in a rented project. Missed: rebuild, keep, or another place.
2. **Scheduled task: rebuild.** Missed: carry.
3. **AP policy version 3: carry, and move to the KSoR**, the approved policies with their versions. It sits in one person's email. Missed: rebuild, keep, or another place.
4. **Connector, accounting system: rebuild.** It is the AI vendor's link, set up again on the next one. Missed: carry.
5. **Run notes: carry, and move to each invoice's case file, the SSoR.** They are the only record of where each invoice stands. Missed: rebuild, keep, or another place.
6. **#ap-help: rebuild.** Missed: carry.

Plain words for a place pass, such as "the approved policies" for the KSoR.

Never a deduction: how a part is worded on the map, or the order of the rows.

## Why

The swap test sorts every part. What comes with the AI vendor's product is rebuilt on the next one: the schedule, the connectors, the channels people use to reach the worker. They are meant to be replaced. What must last is Brightline's own, and it is carried across: the worker's definition, its approved knowledge, and where each piece of work stands. A connector is rebuilt even when it writes to the accounting system: the accounting system stays Brightline's, and only the AI vendor's link to it is set up again. A schedule is rebuilt too, because the business time it serves is written in the Role Contract. All three owned parts move, because each is kept today where Brightline does not control it: the instructions' only copy is in a rented project, version 3 is in one person's email, and the run notes, the only record of what each invoice waits for, exist only in a chat. Leave them behind, and nobody knows where the work stands.

## Take it to work

For each thing your AI setup relies on, ask: if we switched AI vendors, would we rebuild it, or carry it across? Anything you would carry belongs in a place you control.
