# Lab 4 answer key: Task 1. What IT gave you

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the table of IT's 13 parts is in your contract document and your first test is done, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your contract document's words, and reads your test's reply in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

3. **The first test ran, and its reply says which line decided each question, or that none did.** This is the "before": your contract as Brightline had it that week. It is not scored on its answers. Missed only if the test did not run, or its reply names no line and does not say that none decides.

## Why these, and not the others

A part is misplaced when its only copy sits where the company does not govern it, when the worker answers from a copy instead of the home, or when the worker can change a company system with no check between. The project instructions are the worker's definition, kept only in a rented product. Version 1 is a copy that became the home. Version 3 is the home, but it sits in one person's email. Three memory entries hold facts or rules, which memory must never hold. The connector writes with Maria's login. Approvals sit in chat history. The run notes are the story of the work, kept only in a chat.

The four OK parts belong where they are. A preference may live in memory. A schedule and two connections are rented by design, and rebuilding them on another vendor is normal. The scheduled task is OK because Draft 2's Triggers line already holds the business time: the feature that fires is rented.
