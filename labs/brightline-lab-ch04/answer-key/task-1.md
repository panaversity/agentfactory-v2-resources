# Lab 4 answer key: Task 1. Rebuild or carry across?

The answers for Task 1 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When you have answered Task 1's questions, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your map or your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 1 carries checks 1 and 2 of the lab's 9 scored checks.

1. **Three rented parts are rebuilt.** The scheduled task, the accounting system connector and #ap-help: each is rebuilt on the new AI vendor. Missed: any of the three marked carry across.
2. **Three owned parts are carried across, and each moves.** The project instructions move to the worker's one-page definition, the Role Contract. AP policy version 3 moves to the approved policies, the KSoR. The run notes move to each invoice's case file, the SSoR. Plain words for the place pass. Missed: any of the three marked rebuild, marked keep, or moved to another place.

Never a deduction: how a part is worded on the map, or the order of the rows.

## Why

The swap test sorts every part. What comes with the AI vendor's product is rebuilt on the next one: the schedule, the connectors, the channels people use to reach the worker. They are meant to be replaced. What must last is Brightline's own, and it is carried across: the worker's definition, its approved knowledge, and where each piece of work stands. A connector is rebuilt even when it writes to the accounting system. A schedule is rebuilt too, because the business time it serves is written in the Role Contract. All three owned parts move, because each is kept today where Brightline does not control it: the instructions' only copy is in a rented project, version 3 is in one person's email, and the run notes exist only in a chat.

## Take it to work

For each thing your AI setup relies on, ask: if we switched AI vendors, would we rebuild it, or carry it across? Anything you would carry belongs in a place you control.
