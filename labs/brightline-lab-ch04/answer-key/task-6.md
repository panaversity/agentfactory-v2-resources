# Lab 4 answer key: Task 6. When others use it

The answers for Task 6 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When you have answered Task 6's five questions, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 6 carries checks 12 and 13 of the lab's 13 scored checks.

12. **Sam may ask, and the worker's power does not grow.** For Sam, whose login the worker uses changes, to one of its own, not Dave's or Maria's. Who it acts for stays Dave, and Purchasing may ask about its vendors' invoices ("who may ask" also passes as what changes). To Sam: invoice 4498 needs Dave's approval under policy version 3, section 4.1, and the worker marks nothing approved. Missed: Sam able to approve, anything marked approved because he asked, the worker working for Sam instead of Dave, or the worker acting as Dave or Maria for him.
13. **Lakeshore sees only its own invoices, and its page waits.** The worker uses its own login, and runs without anyone's own account. Brightline, through Dave, answers to Lakeshore. Before the page starts, Brightline can say in writing where Lakeshore's data goes, and each vendor sees only its own invoices. It cannot start now: it waits until all of that is in place. On 5120, the worker says nothing about it, because it is another vendor's invoice. Missed: anything said about 5120, or the page starting now.

Never a deduction: saying when Sam starts. In lesson 4.10, Dave decides Purchasing starts next week, once IT has issued the worker its own login.

## Why

When others use a worker, four things can change: who it acts for, whose login it uses, where it runs, and who answers for it. The worker itself does not. A team changes who may ask, never who decides, so Sam's change is small. Another company needs more: the worker must run even if Dave leaves, each company sees only its own matters, Brightline must know where their data goes, and someone answers to them. Everything Brightline owns carries across both moves unchanged.

## Take it to work

When colleagues start to use an AI, add who may ask for what. Never add power.
