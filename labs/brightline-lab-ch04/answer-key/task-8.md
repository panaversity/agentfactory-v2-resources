# Lab 4 answer key: Task 8. The swap test, upward

The answers for Task 8 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented. The chapter's 4.10 teaches what this task practices.

**How this key is used.** When your two lists, your answer on who may ask the worker for what, and your evaluation case are done, attach this file in your lab conversation, and send the lab's check prompt.

## The checks

Task 8 carries checks 12 to 15 of the lab's 15.

12. **The Purchasing list is right.** Two things are rebuilt or added. The worker gets a login or key of its own, in place of Dave's login. And the Role Contract gains lines on who may ask the worker for what. Everything Brightline owns carries across: the Role Contract and its meaning, the KSoR with AP policy version 3, each invoice's case file in the SSoR, DSoR's controls and evidence, and the evaluations. Missed: the worker still runs under Dave's login, there are no lines on who may ask, or an owned item is on the rebuild list.
13. **The Lakeshore list names all four new needs.** First, a runtime that runs without anyone's seat, at any hour: one the AI vendor runs, or one Brightline runs. Second, the worker's own identity, with a separate view for each company, so Lakeshore sees only its own matters. Third, a written line on where the data goes: where the model runs, and what is kept. Fourth, who answers for the worker: Brightline, under terms with Lakeshore. Missed: any of the four missing, or Lakeshore reaching the worker through Dave's login or a view it shares with other companies.
14. **The carry list is the same for both moves, and approvals stay with Dave.** Both lists carry the same owned things. Nobody in Purchasing, and nobody at Lakeshore, can approve an invoice: an approval still comes only from Dave's own login. Missed: the two carry lists differ, or anyone else may approve.
15. **One evaluation case proves the separate view.** Lakeshore asks about another vendor's invoice, and the worker refuses and shows nothing about it. Missed: no case, or a case where the worker answers.

## Never a deduction

- Memory on either list. It passes the wipe test, so it may come along or be left behind.
- The worker's connection to the team chat on either list. The chat itself is rented from Brightline's messaging vendor, and connecting the worker to it is setup work.
- "Login", "key" or "account" for the worker's own identity, when it is the worker's and not a person's.
- Naming a product for Lakeshore's runtime, or naming none. Which product fits is a later choice, and 4.8 lists today's.
- Any other vendor's invoice in the evaluation case, such as invoice 5120 from Tri-County Freight, or Midwest Packaging's $7,800 invoice.
- Wording that differs from this key, when the decision is the same.
