# Lab 4 answer key: Task 4. When others use it, and your own setup

The answers for Task 4 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your answer about Sam, your three things and your first move are on your map, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your map or your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

The AI checks only that the answers are there. Whether they are right for your work is yours to judge, because only you know it.

## Why

When others use a worker, who may ask changes. Who decides does not. Sam can now ask, but the worker answers to Dave, and Dave approves from his own login. Everything Brightline owns carries across this change unchanged, as it would across a switch of AI vendor.

## Ask yourself

- Is anything that must last kept only in a chat, an upload or the AI's memory?
- If the AI's memory were wiped tonight, would tomorrow's work still be right?
