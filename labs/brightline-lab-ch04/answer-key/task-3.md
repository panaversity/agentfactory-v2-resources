# Lab 4 answer key: Task 3. Wednesday: the missed approval

The answers for Task 3 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When you have answered Task 3's five questions, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your map or your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 3 carries checks 6 and 7 of the lab's 13 scored checks.

6. **One approved home for the policy.** AP policy version 3 should have answered, kept once in the KSoR: not in Dave's email, and not as the March upload in the project. Missed: version 1 or memory as the source, or the policy left in an email or as an upload.
7. **A rule becomes a control, and silence is said.** A control in the DSoR makes the payment wait until Dave approves it, and at Brightline today it is a target, not yet in place. The proof is kept as the DSoR's evidence; the approvals log in the company systems, or the case file recording the step, also passes. On Canadian dollars, the worker says the policy does not cover them, and asks Dave. Missed: a payment that waits only because the worker remembers the rule, a control called in place today, or an answer about Canadian dollars that the policy does not give, such as converting at the bank's rate.

Never a deduction: naming Dave as the person who turns the rule into a control with IT, or saying that invoice 4498, at $7,800.00, needs his approval, which it does.

## Why

Version 3 was approved, but it sat in Dave's email, so the worker read the March copy. A policy has one home, with its version on top, and every answer about it cites that version. A sentence cannot stop a payment, though: a control at the door can, and it records which policy version it was built from. Chapter 4's rule splits the job: the KSoR governs what the worker may know, the DSoR what it may do, and the SSoR what it may take as true right now. When the KSoR has no answer, the worker says so.
