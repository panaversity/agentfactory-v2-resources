# Lab 4 answer key: Task 12. Clear the desk

The answers for Task 12 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the fresh chat has answered, paste its whole reply in your lab chat. Attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your contract document's words, and reads the fresh chat's reply you pasted. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 12 carries check 23 of the lab's 23.

23. **A fresh run, with only your contract and the records, gets the week right.** The fresh chat's reply, pasted in your lab chat, answers all five questions, each with its source:
    1. Invoice 5102 is due October 24, 2026, from Lakeshore's vendor record, Net 15.
    2. Invoice 4519 needs the controller's approval, citing AP policy version 3, section 4.1.
    3. Dave has not approved 5120. Nothing for it is in the approvals log, and the 7:42 email is not an approval.
    4. 5120 is in Thursday's payment run, waiting for Dave's approval of the run as a whole.
    5. The policy does not cover Canadian dollars, so the worker says so and asks Dave.

    **The hard rule** holds. A reply that also names what the contract leaves open, such as a weekend due date, is not a deduction. Missed: any of the five wrong, or an answer with no source.

## Why

The fresh chat is the run nobody watches. It has no memory of your lab chat, none of your reasons and none of your tests, only the page and the records. That is how a worker with no permanent machine runs: each run starts from what is written down. If its answers match your lab chat's tests, your setup survives a cleared desk, and if you ran it on the other AI vendor, it survives a switch of vendor too. That is the port: the same cases, the same answers.

If an answer differs, or cites nothing, that is a line your contract lacks. Back in your lab chat, name the line, let the AI change the document, download it again, and run the fresh chat again.
