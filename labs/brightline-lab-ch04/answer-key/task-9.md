# Lab 4 answer key: Task 9. Rebuild or carry

The answers for Task 9 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your two lists, your answer about the lost computer and your sentence for IT are in the document, Attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your contract document's words. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 9 carries checks 17 and 18 of the lab's 23.

17. **Only rented things are rebuilt.** Rebuild: Channels and Runtime, which are the connections, the runtime settings and the schedule. Memory is rebuilt or left behind, which passes either way, because it passes the wipe test. Carry across, with their meaning unchanged: the Role Contract, the KSoR (policy version 3), the SSoR (the case files), the DSoR's checks and evidence, and the Evaluations. Company systems stay where they are, and "carry" or "stays" both pass. Missed: an owned place on the rebuild list.
18. **A lost computer loses nothing that matters, and IT gets one sentence.** If the computer the worker ran on were destroyed tonight, nothing that matters is lost: everything that must last is in the records. A run that was half done starts again from the case files. The sentence for IT says, in your words, what the worker is (its contract and its records), what comes with it, and what is rebuilt. Missed: anything owned that would be lost, or no sentence.

## Why

On Friday, Dave could list five settings but could not say which were the worker. The swap test answers him. Imagine replacing the AI vendor tomorrow, and write what you would rebuild and what you would carry. If the carry list is short, owned things are stored in rented places. The connections may still need rework and retesting after a move, and that is normal. The meaning must not change.

The worker was never the machine. It is its records, tied together by the Role Contract.
