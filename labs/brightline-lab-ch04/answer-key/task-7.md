# Lab 4 answer key: Task 7. Your own setup

The answers for Task 7 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition.

**How this key is used.** When your own page is in the chat and "Move my setup to the new vendor" is back, attach this file, and send the check prompt. Check 22 is not scored: nobody but you knows your setup. The AI reads your page against it and tells you what it sees. You have the final say.

## The check

22. **Each of your three things has a layer and a place, and the one the move loses is the one you would miss most.** Your page names three things your own AI use relies on, and for each: which of the six layers it sits in, whether it is rented or owned, and where it should live. The run of the move on your page says which of the three the new setup has and which was lost.
    - Read the move. For each thing lost: is it one you would miss on Monday? If yes, your page kept it where the move cannot carry it, and the fix is a place you control: a file, a folder your team keeps, a line in a document.
    - For each thing kept: did the page say where it comes from? If the move says "nothing on the page decides", it was kept by luck.

## Why

Chapter 4's picture is for any worker, including the one you already are to your AI. A prompt you keep in a chat is in a rented runtime; a file you uploaded is knowledge in a rented project; what the AI remembers about you is memory, on the line. Each is fine until you change vendors, and then only what you kept somewhere of your own comes with you.

## Take it to work

Move the one thing you would miss most, today, to a place you control. Then the move, when it comes, is a rebuild.
