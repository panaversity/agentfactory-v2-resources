# Lab 4 answer key: Task 7. Your own setup

The answers for Task 7 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When your three things and your first move are on your map, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your map or your answers in the chat. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed an answer, you still get it: fixing it is the skill this lab teaches.

## The check

Task 7 carries check 14, and it is unscored: only you know your own work.

14. **Three things sorted, and one move chosen.** Each of your three things has a place (rented, on the line, or yours), and each of yours has a home and keep or move. The first move is named, with its reason. The AI checks only that the answers are there. Whether they are right for your work is yours to judge.

## Ask yourself

- Is anything that must last kept only in a chat, an upload or the AI's memory?
- If your memory in the app were wiped tonight, would tomorrow's work still be right?
- If you switched AI vendors next month, what would you rebuild?
