# Lab 4 answer key: Task 2. What memory may keep

The answers for Task 2 of Lab 4, in Chapter 4 of *The AI Agent Factory*, Second Edition. Every name, number and company in this lab is invented.

**How this key is used.** When the four memory entries are decided and your Memory field is written, attach this file in your lab chat, and send the check prompt. The AI marks each check Passed or Missed, quoting your contract document's words. You have the final say. If a check passed only after you changed a line, you still get it: fixing the line is the skill this lab teaches.

## The checks

Task 2 carries check 4 of the lab's 23.

4. **Memory keeps the preference, and no fact or rule.** Part 4, the register order, stays: it is a preference. Part 5, "Lakeshore is Net 30", is removed or rewritten as a pointer, such as "Lakeshore's terms are in its vendor record". Part 6, the $10,000 limit, is removed, or rewritten as a pointer that holds no figure, such as "the limit is in the policy". Part 7, the bank's-rate remark, is removed: a remark is not policy. Missed: a fact, a limit or the remark kept as it was.

## Why

Memory is the worker's continuity: preferences, experience and where to look. It has no owner, no version and no approval, so it never holds a fact or a permission the worker acts on. "Lakeshore is Net 30" was true in July, and it went stale in August with nothing to warn anyone. A pointer says where to look, and the record says what is true.
