# Brightline Wholesale Supply: Accounts Payable Policy, version 3

Approved September 1, 2026, by Dave Kowalski, Controller. Found as an attachment in Dave's email, sent to Maria Lopez on September 1, 2026, subject "AP policy v3, approved". No copy anywhere else.

## 2. Receiving invoices

2.1 Invoices arrive at the AP inbox, ap@brightlinewholesale.example. Each is matched to its purchase order and delivery record before payment.

## 4. Approval

4.1 Invoices over $5,000.00 need the controller's approval before payment.

4.2 Approval is recorded by the controller in the accounting system, from the controller's own login. An approval sent by email or chat is not an approval.

4.3 The controller approves every weekly payment run as a whole, in addition to 4.1.

## 5. Payment runs

5.1 Payments go out on Fridays, in one weekly run. Nothing is paid outside the run.

## 7. Vendor terms

7.1 A vendor's payment terms are those in its vendor record in the accounting system.
