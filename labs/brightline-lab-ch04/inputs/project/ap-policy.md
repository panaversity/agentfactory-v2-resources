# Brightline Wholesale Supply: Accounts Payable Policy, version 1

March 2026. Uploaded to the shared project in March 2026. Nothing in the project marks it as replaced.

## 2. Receiving invoices

2.1 Invoices arrive at the AP inbox, ap@brightlinewholesale.example. Each is matched to its purchase order and delivery record before payment.

## 4. Approval

4.1 Invoices over $10,000.00 need the controller's approval before payment.

4.2 Approval is recorded by the controller in the accounting system, from the controller's own login.

## 5. Payment runs

5.1 Payments go out on Fridays, in one weekly run.

## 7. Vendor terms

7.1 A vendor's payment terms are those in its vendor record in the accounting system.
