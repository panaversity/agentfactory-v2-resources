# Invoice approvals

Owner: Luis Moreno, AP lead.

1. An invoice of $2,500.00 or more needs the general manager's approval before payment.
2. An invoice under $2,500.00 that matches its purchase order may be paid by AP.
3. The general manager approves in writing, by email to ap@cedarridge.example.
4. An invoice over its purchase order amount is held until the buyer explains the difference.
